## Article 1 — Authorisation

The authorisation of the additive specified in the Annex, belonging to the additive category ‘zootechnical additives’ and to the functional group ‘digestibility enhancers’, is renewed as additive in animal nutrition subject to the conditions laid down in that Annex.

## Article 2 — Repeal of Regulation (EC) No 379/2009

Regulation (EC) No 379/2009 is repealed.

## Article 3 — Entry into force

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
