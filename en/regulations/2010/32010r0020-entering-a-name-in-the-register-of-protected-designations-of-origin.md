# Commission Regulation (EU) No 20/2010 of 12 January 2010 entering a name in the register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications [Arzúa-Ulloa (PDO)]

**COMMISSION REGULATION (EU) No 20/2010**

**of 12 January 2010**

**entering a name in the register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications [ Arzúa-Ulloa  (PDO)]**

**COMMISSION REGULATION (EU) No 20/2010**

**of 12 January 2010**

**entering a name in the register of protected designations of origin and protected geographical indications [ Arzúa-Ulloa  (PDO)]**

## Article 1

The name contained in the Annex to this Regulation is hereby entered in the Register.

## Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

## ANNEX

Agricultural products intended for human consumption listed in Annex I to the Treaty:

**Class 1.3. Cheeses**

**SPAIN**

Arzúa-Ulloa  (PDO)
