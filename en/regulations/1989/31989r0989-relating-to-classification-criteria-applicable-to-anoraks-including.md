**COMMISSION REGULATION (EEC) No 989/89**

**of 17 April 1989**

**relating to classification criteria applicable to anoraks (including ski-jackets), wind-cheaters, wind-jackets and similar articles of CN codes 6101 , 6102 , 6201 and 6202**

## Article 1

Only garments which have long sleeves are classified as anoraks (including ski-jackets), wind-cheaters, wind-jackets and similar articles falling within CN codes 6101 , 6102 , 6201 and 6202 .

By derogation from the first paragraph those headings shall include padded waistcoats, despite the fact that they have no sleeves.

## Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force 21 days after its publication in the Official Journal of the European Communities.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
