# Commission Regulation (EEC) No 1460/81 of 26 May 1981 specifying the extent to which applications lodged during the month of May 1981 for import licences in respect of young male bovine animals for fattening may be accepted

Commission regulation (EEC) No 1460/81

of 26 May 1981

specifying the extent to which applications lodged during the month of May 1981 for import licences in respect of young male bovine animals for fattening may be accepted

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Economic Community,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EEC) No 805/68 of 27 June 1968 on the common organization of the market in beef and veal [1], as last amended by the Act of Accession of Greece [2], and in particular Article 13 (4) (a) thereof,

Whereas Commission Regulation (EEC) No 977/81 [3] fixed the quantity of young male bovine animals which may be imported on special terms during the second quarter of 1981; whereas, having regard to the applications for import licences lodged by each of the categories of applicants referred to in that Regulation, such licences should be issued as provided below;

Whereas the measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Management Committee for Beef and Veal,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION :

## Article 1

Import licences for young male bovine animals for fattening in respect of which applications were lodged between 30 April and 8 May 1981 shall be issued as follows :

1\. The quantities requested in Italy :

(a) for animals of 220 to 300 kilograms per capita live weight coming from Yugoslavia :

(aa) by agricultural producers or their organizations shall be reduced by 78,009 %,

(bb) by other applicants shall be reduced by 95,833 % ;

(b) for animals of up to 300 kilograms per capita live weight coming from other non-member countries:

(aa) by agricultural producers, or their organizations shall be reduced by 91,215 %

(bb) by other applicants shall be reduced by 98,141 %.

2\. The quantities requested in the other Member States :

(a) for animals of 220 to 300 kilograms per capita live weight coming from Yugoslavia shall be reduced by 90,731 % ;

(b) for animals of up to 300 kilograms per capita live weight coming from other non-member countries shall be reduced by 98,149 %.

## Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on 30 May 1981.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 26 May 1981.

For the Commission

Poul Dalsager

Member of the Commission
