# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/431 of 5 March 2025 correcting Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/120 imposing a definitive anti-dumping duty on imports of electric bicycles, originating in the People’s Republic of China following an expiry review pursuant to Article 11(2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/1036 of the European Parliament and of the Council

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2025/431**

**of 5 March 2025**

**correcting Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/120 imposing a definitive anti-dumping duty on imports of electric bicycles, originating in the People’s Republic of China following an expiry review pursuant to Article 11(2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/1036 of the European Parliament and of the Council**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2016/1036 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 June 2016 on protection against dumped imports from countries not members of the European Union (1), and in particular Article 11(2) thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Due to an error, Annexes I and II listing other cooperating companies in the original anti-dumping investigation, as referred to in Article 1(2) of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/120 (2), were duplicated from Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/114 (3) published on the same day concerning the parallel anti-subsidy investigation. These two Annexes need to be replaced in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/120 to ensure a proper implementation of that act.

(2) Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/120 should therefore be corrected accordingly. Annex III of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/120 is correct and should remain unchanged.

(3) This Regulation should apply with effect from the date of entry into force of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/120, in order to ensure the proper application of that Implementing Regulation to all exporting producers that are subject to the anti-dumping duty.

(4) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Article 15(1) of Regulation (EU) 2016/1036,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

## Article 1

In Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/120, Annexes I and II are replaced with Annexes I and II to this Regulation.

## Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

It shall apply from 25 January 2025.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 5 March 2025.

For the Commission

The President

Ursula VON DER LEYEN
