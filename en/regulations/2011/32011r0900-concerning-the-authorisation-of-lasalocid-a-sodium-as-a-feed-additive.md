## Article 1

The preparation specified in the Annex, belonging to the additive category ‘coccdiostats and histomonostats’ is authorised as an additive in animal nutrition, subject to the conditions laid down in that Annex.

## Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
