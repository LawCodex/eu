# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2162 of 29 September 2026 making imports of solid glass microspheres (commercially known as glass beads) originating in the People’s Republic of China subject to registration with a view to allowing the levy of anti-dumping duties on the imports subject to registration

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2026/2162**

**of 29 September 2026**

**making imports of solid glass microspheres (commercially known as ‘glass beads’) originating in the People’s Republic of China subject to registration with a view to allowing the levy of anti-dumping duties on the imports subject to registration**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2016/1036 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 June 2016 on protection against dumped imports from countries not members of the European Union (1) (‘the basic Regulation’) and in particular Article 14(5) thereof,

After informing the Member States,

Whereas:

(1) On 16 July 2026, the European Commission (‘the Commission’) announced, by a notice published in the Official Journal of the European Union (2), the initiation of an anti-dumping proceeding with regard to imports into the Union of solid glass microspheres (commercially known as ‘glass beads’) originating in the People’s Republic of China.

(2) This initiation followed a complaint lodged on 24 June 2026 by M. Swarovski GmbH, Swarco Vestglas GmbH, Swarco Schönborn GmbH, Sovitec Belgium, Potters Ballotini SASZ, Sovitec France, Potters Ballotini Gmbh, Intermiglass SP. Z O.O. and Sovitec Iberica SAU on behalf of the Union industry representing more than 25 % of the total Union production of solid glass microspheres .

**1\. PRODUCT SUBJECT TO REGISTRATION**

(3) The product subject to registration (‘the product concerned’) is solid glass microspheres (commercially known as ‘glass beads’) not exceeding 2 mm in diameter, with surface either coated or uncoated, irrespective of the intended use or production process, currently falling within CN codes ex 7002 10 00 , ex 7018 10 90 , ex 7018 20 00 and ex 7018 90 90 (TARIC codes 7002 10 00 20, 7018 10 90 10, 7018 20 00 10 and 7018 90 90 10) and originating in the People’s Republic of China.

(4) The product concerned does not include products defined as ‘glass beads’ that have the form of small pierced balls, currently falling within CN codes 7018 10 11 and 7018 10 19 .

(5) The Commission clarified that glass spheres that meet the specifications set out in the product definition are part of the product scope of the investigation, regardless of whether or not they are classified under CN codes ex 7002 10 00 , ex 7018 10 90 , ex 7018 20 00 and ex 7018 90 90 (TARIC codes 7002 10 00 20, 7018 10 90 10, 7018 20 00 10 and 7018 90 90 10).

**2\. REGISTRATION**

(6) Under Article 14(5) of the basic Regulation, imports of the product concerned may be made subject to registration.

(7) The purpose of registration is to ensure that anti-dumping duties, if any, can be levied retroactively on the imports subject to registration in accordance with the applicable legal provisions, if the necessary conditions are met.

(8) The Commission has decided to make imports of the product concerned subject to registration on its own initiative under Article 14(5) of the basic Regulation. The conditions for retroactive collection of duties will be assessed in the regulation imposing definitive duties if any.

(9) Any future liability would emanate from the findings of the anti-dumping investigation.

(10) The calculations provided in the complaint requesting the initiation of an anti-dumping investigation estimate a dumping margin of 77,48 % and an injury elimination level of 125 % for the product concerned for the period from 1 January 2022 to 31 December 2025. The amount of possible future liability would normally be set at the lower of the dumping or injury level according to Article 9(4) of the basic Regulation.

(11) However, at this stage the Commission is not in a position to estimate the amount of possible future liability. Thus, the amounts mentioned in the complaint are only for information purposes and cannot create any expectations as to the actual level of liability which will be established as a result of the investigation.

(12) If, during the investigation, the Commission finds evidence of raw material distortions pursuant to Article 7(2a) of the basic Regulation, the amount of possible future liability would be set at the level of the dumping margin as provided for in Article 7(2b) of the basic Regulation if it is concluded that a duty lower than the margin of dumping would not be sufficient to remove the injury suffered by the Union industry.

**3\. PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA**

(13) Any personal data collected in the context of this registration will be treated in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 of the European Parliament and of the Council (3),

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

## Article 1

1\. The customs authorities are hereby directed, under Article 14(5) of Regulation (EU) 2016/1036, to take the appropriate steps to register imports into the Union of solid glass microspheres (commercially known as ‘glass beads’) not exceeding 2 mm in diameter, with surface either coated or uncoated, irrespective of the intended use or production process, currently falling within CN codes ex 7002 10 00 , ex 7018 10 90 , ex 7018 20 00 and ex 7018 90 90 (TARIC codes 7002 10 00 20, 7018 10 90 10, 7018 20 00 10 and 7018 90 90 10) and originating in the People’s Republic of China.

The product made subject to registration does not include products defined as ‘glass beads’ that have the form of small pierced balls, currently falling within CN codes 7018 10 11 and 7018 10 19 .

2\. Registration shall expire nine months following the date of entry into force of this Regulation.

## Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 29 September 2026.

For the Commission

The President
