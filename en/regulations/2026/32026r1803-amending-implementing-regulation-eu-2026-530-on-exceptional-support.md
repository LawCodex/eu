# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1803 of 24 July 2026 amending Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/530 on exceptional support measures for the sheepmeat and pigmeat sectors in Hungary and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1100 on exceptional support measures for the milk and pigmeat sectors in Slovakia

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2026/1803**

**of 24 July 2026**

**amending Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/530 on exceptional support measures for the sheepmeat and pigmeat sectors in Hungary and Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1100 on exceptional support measures for the milk and pigmeat sectors in Slovakia**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007 (1), and in particular Article 220(1), first subparagraph, point (a), thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Within the context of outbreaks of foot and mouth diseases in Hungary and Slovakia in the spring of 2025, the Commission adopted Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/530 (2) providing for exceptional support measures for the sheepmeat and pigmeat sectors in Hungary and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1100 (3) providing for exceptional support measures for the milk and pigmeat sectors in Slovakia.

(2) Following parliamentary elections held in Hungary on 12 April 2026 and the subsequent government transition, the adoption of legislative measures required to fulfil the eligibility criteria under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/530 have been delayed. In light of the principle of sincere cooperation laid down in Article 4(3) of the Treaty on European Union, it is justified to grant Hungary a time-limited extension of the eligibility date until 30 September 2026. This will enable the completion of outstanding administrative steps without compromising the Union’s financial interests, while ensuring Hungary remains accountable for swift progress in adopting the legislative measures.

(3) Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1100 sets 31 October 2026 as the date before which expenditure borne by Slovakia is to be paid in order to be eligible for Union part-financing. Respect of the eligibility date of 31 October cannot be controlled as expenditure effected between 16 and 31 October are to be counted as having been made in the month of November in accordance with Article 21(2) of Regulation (EU) 2021/2116 of the European Parliament and of the Council (4) and are to be declared as an aggregated amount in the declaration of that month. To ensure the effective verification of expenditure in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2021/2116, it is appropriate to change the eligibility date for Union part-financing to 30 November 2026.

(4) In the interest of clarity and consistency with previous similar measures, where the flat rates indicated correspond to the Union co-financing part, for losses related to the prolonged rearing and fattening periods of animals set out in Article 3(1), point (a), of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/530 and in Article 3(1), points (b), (c) and (d), of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1100 the flat rates should be adjusted to match the Union co-financing part of 60 % of the total expenditure.

(5) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee for the Common Organisation of the Agricultural Markets,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

## Article 1 — Amendments to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/530

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/530 is amended as follows:

(1) Article 2 is amended as follows:

(a) in paragraph 1, point (c) is replaced by the following:

‘(c) if it has been paid by Hungary to the beneficiaries by 30 September 2026; and’;

(b) paragraph 2 is replaced by the following:

‘2. None of the expenditure paid by Hungary after 30 September 2026 shall be eligible for Union financing, irrespective of the share of the expenditure it represents.’

;

(2) in Article 3(1), point (a) is replaced by the following:

‘(a) for losses related to the prolonged rearing and fattening periods of pigs due to movement restrictions in the regulated zones, the flat rate EUR 0,708 per pig per day up to a maximum of 28 441 animals shall apply;’.

## Article 2 — Amendments to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1100

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1100 is amended as follows:

(1) Article 2 is amended as follows:

(a) in paragraph 1, point (c) is replaced by the following:

‘(c) if it has been paid by Slovakia to the beneficiaries by 30 November 2026; and’;

(b) paragraph 2 is replaced by the following:

‘2. None of the expenditure paid by Slovakia after 30 November 2026 shall be eligible for Union part-financing, irrespective of the share of the expenditure it represents.’

;

(2) in Article 3(1), points (b), (c) and (d) are replaced by the following:

‘(b) for losses related to the prolonged rearing and fattening periods of pigs due to movement restrictions in the regulated zones, a flat rate of EUR 0,786 per pig per day up to a maximum of 76 673 animals;

(c) for losses related to the prolonged rearing periods of piglets due to movement restrictions in the regulated zones, a flat rate EUR 0,48 per piglet per day up to a maximum of 79 198 animals;

(d) for losses related to the prolonged rearing periods of sows resulting from movement restrictions in the regulated zones: EUR 2,016 per sow per day up to a maximum of 1 025 animals for the period between 21 March 2025 and 30 March 2025 and EUR 2,508 per sow per day up to maximum of 1 593 animals for the period between 31 March 2025 and 20 May 2025.’.

## Article 3 — Entry into force

This Regulation shall enter into force on the third day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 24 July 2026.

For the Commission
