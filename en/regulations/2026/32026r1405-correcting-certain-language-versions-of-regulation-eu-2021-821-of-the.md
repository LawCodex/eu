# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1405 of 26 June 2026 correcting certain language versions of Regulation (EU) 2021/821 of the European Parliament and of the Council setting up a Union regime for the control of exports, brokering, technical assistance, transit and transfer of dual-use items

**COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) 2026/1405**

**of 26 June 2026**

**correcting certain language versions of Regulation (EU) 2021/821 of the European Parliament and of the Council setting up a Union regime for the control of exports, brokering, technical assistance, transit and transfer of dual-use items**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2021/821 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 May 2021 setting up a Union regime for the control of exports, brokering, technical assistance, transit and transfer of dual-use items (1), and in particular Article 17(1), point (a), thereof,

Whereas:

(1) The Hungarian, Lithuanian, Slovak and Swedish language versions of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2021/821 contain errors in Part I, in the definition of nuclear reactor. The errors were introduced by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/1 (2) and affect the substance of that definition.

(2) The Dutch, Greek, Slovenian and Swedish language versions of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2021/821 contain an error in Part IV, point 2B510.b.2 as regards the characteristics of the vacuum. The error was introduced by Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2003 (3) and affects the substance of that provision.

(3) The Irish and Swedish language versions of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2021/821 also contain an error in Part V, point 3B501.n.4 as regards the type of metal. The error was introduced by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2003 and affects the substance of that provision.

(4) In addition, the Swedish language version of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2021/821 contains an error in Part V, point 3B501.n.7.b.1 as regards the type of filter. The error was introduced by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2003 and affects the substance of that provision.

(5) The Dutch, Greek, Hungarian, Irish, Lithuanian, Slovak, Slovenian and Swedish language versions of Regulation (EU) 2021/821 should therefore be corrected accordingly. The other language versions are not affected,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

## Article 1

(Does not concern the English language.)

## Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 26 June 2026.

For the Commission

The President

Ursula VON DER LEYEN
