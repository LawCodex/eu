# Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1174 of 28 May 2026 implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/386 establishing restrictive measures against those who support, facilitate or enable violent actions by Hamas and the Palestinian Islamic Jihad

**COUNCIL IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2026/1174**

**of 28 May 2026**

**implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/386 establishing restrictive measures against those who support, facilitate or enable violent actions by Hamas and the Palestinian Islamic Jihad**

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EU) 2024/386 of 19 January 2024 establishing restrictive measures against those who support, facilitate or enable violent actions by Hamas and the Palestinian Islamic Jihad (1), and in particular Article 13(1) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

(1) On 19 January 2024, the Council adopted Regulation (EU) 2024/386.

(2) In view of the continued threat posed by Hamas and the Palestinian Islamic Jihad, ten persons should be added to the list of natural or legal persons, groups, entities and bodies set out in Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2024/386.

(3) Regulation (EU) 2024/386 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

## Article 1

Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2024/386 is amended in accordance with the Annex to this Regulation.

## Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 28 May 2026.

For the Council

The President

M. DAMIANOS

(1) OJ L, 2024/386, 19.1.2024, ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/386/oj.
