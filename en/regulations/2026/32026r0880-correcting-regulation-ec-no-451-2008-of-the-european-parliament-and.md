# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/880 of 11 December 2025 correcting Regulation (EC) No 451/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards specific positions of the classification of products by activity (CPA)

**COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) 2026/880**

**of 11 December 2025**

**correcting Regulation (EC) No 451/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards specific positions of the classification of products by activity (CPA)**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EC) No 451/2008 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 April 2008 establishing a new statistical classification of products by activity (CPA) and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 3696/93 (1), and in particular Article 6(2) thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/3103 (2) amended Regulation (EC) No 451/2008 in order to establish Version 2.2 of the statistical classification of products by activity (CPA Ver. 2.2).

(2) However, by oversight, a number of structural errors were made in CPA Ver. 2.2. Those errors need to be corrected in order to avoid errors in the transmission of data from Member States to the Commission (Eurostat).

(3) Regulation (EC) No 451/2008 should therefore be corrected accordingly.

(4) In order to ensure consistency, as regards data referred to in Annex B to Regulation (EU) No 549/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council (3), the date of application of this Regulation should be aligned with the date of application of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/3103,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

## Article 1

The Annex to Regulation (EC) No 451/2008 is corrected as set out in the Annex to this Regulation.

## Article 2

As regards data referred to in Annex B to Regulation (EU) No 549/2013, this Regulation shall apply from 1 September 2029.

## Article 3

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 11 December 2025.

For the Commission

The President

Ursula VON DER LEYEN
