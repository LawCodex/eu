# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/971 of 4 May 2026 supplementing Directive 2004/109/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to regulatory technical standards on access to regulated information at Union level and repealing Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1437

**COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) 2026/971**

**of 4 May 2026**

**supplementing Directive 2004/109/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to regulatory technical standards on access to regulated information at Union level and repealing Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1437**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Directive 2004/109/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 December 2004 on the harmonisation of transparency requirements in relation to information about issuers whose securities are admitted to trading on a regulated market and amending Directive 2001/34/EC (1), and in particular Article 22(2), third subparagraph, thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Regulation (EU) 2023/2859 of the European Parliament and of the Council (2) requires the European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) to establish and operate a European single access point (ESAP) providing central electronic access to a wide range of information, which includes regulated information submitted by the Officially Appointed Mechanism (OAM) referred to in Article 21(1) and designated under Article 21(2) of Directive 2004/109/EC.

(2) Furthermore, Directive (EU) 2023/2864 of the European Parliament and of the Council (3) repealed Article 21a of Directive 2004/109/EC, under which ESMA operated a web portal serving as a European electronic access point (EEAP) for the storage and publication of such regulated information based on the OAM.

(3) Directive (EU) 2023/2864 also inserted Article 23a into Directive 2004/109/EC to specify that the collection body as defined in Article 2, point (2), of Regulation (EU) 2023/2859 is the OAM designated under Article 21(2) of Directive 2004/109/EC.

(4) As a result, ESAP should now serve the function of giving access, at Union level, to regulated information stored by the OAMs.

(5) To ensure that this Regulation reflects the relevant amendments to Directive 2004/109/EC and is aligned with Regulation (EU) 2023/2859, Directive (EU) 2023/2864 and Commission Implementing Regulations (EU) 2025/1338 (4) and (EU) 2025/1339 (5) on access to regulated information under Directive 2004/109/EC, Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1437 (6) should consequently be amended.

(6) However, as it is necessary to substantially amend all the provisions of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1437, for reasons of legal certainty and to ensure ease of reference, that Delegated Regulation should be repealed and replaced by updated regulatory technical standards which are aligned with the requirements concerning OAMs laid down in Regulation (EU) 2023/2859, Directive (EU) 2023/2864 and Implementing Regulations (EU) 2025/1338 and (EU) 2025/1339.

(7) This Regulation is based on the draft regulatory technical standards submitted to the Commission by ESMA.

(8) ESMA has conducted open public consultations on the draft regulatory technical standards on which this Regulation is based, analysed the potential related costs and benefits and requested the advice of the Securities and Markets Stakeholder Group established by Article 37 of Regulation (EU) No 1095/2010 of the European Parliament and of the Council (7).

(9) Having regard to the fact that, in accordance with Article 23a of Directive 2004/109/EC, Member States are to ensure that regulated information is submitted to the collection body for the purpose of making it accessible on ESAP from 10 July 2026, this Regulation should apply from that date,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

## Article 1 — Search for regulated information

1\. ESMA shall establish and operate the European Single Access Point (‘ESAP’) pursuant to Article 1(1) of Regulation (EU) 2023/2859 as the central access point for the search for regulated information at Union level by users.

2\. ESMA shall ensure that ESAP offers, regarding the regulated information made available by the officially appointed mechanisms (OAMs) referred to in Directive 2004/109/EC, the search criteria referred to in Article 7(3) of Regulation (EU) 2023/2859.

## Article 2 — Communication technologies

1\. ESMA shall guarantee the security and integrity of the regulated information exchanged between OAMs and ESAP.

2\. OAMs shall use the secure internet protocols referred to in Article 4, point (d), of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339, to make information available on ESAP.

3\. OAMs shall make the regulated information available on ESAP via file transfer.

4\. Each OAM shall ensure that its connection with ESAP is available to users at least 97 % per month.

## Article 3 — Provision of information to ESAP by OAMs

1\. Each OAM shall provide to ESAP the regulated information referred to in Article 5(1), point (e), of Regulation (EU) 2023/2859 and within the time limits set out in Article 6 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339.

2\. OAMs shall provide to ESAP the metadata referred to in Article 5(1) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339, including all the metadata that issuers submit to the OAMs pursuant to Article 23a of Directive 2004/109/EC.

3\. Each OAM shall make available to ESAP all language versions of the documents that are disseminated by issuers and stored by the OAM in accordance with Article 21(1) of Directive 2004/109/EC.

4\. Where issuers rectify and resubmit information to an OAM, that OAM shall make available to ESAP the resubmitted document and the accompanying metadata within the time limits set out in Article 6 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339.

5\. OAMs shall not charge ESMA for the delivery of regulated information, the metadata or, where required, the qualified electronic seal, nor for any cost the OAMs incur to connect to ESAP.

## Article 4 — Unique identifier used by OAMs

Each OAM shall use the specific legal entity identifier referred to in Article 2 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1338.

## Article 5 — Common format for the delivery of metadata

1\. Each OAM shall deliver metadata to ESAP in the format specified in Article 5(2) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339.

2\. Each OAM shall deliver metadata on regulated information to ESAP in accordance with the table set out in the Annex to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339.

## Article 6 — Common list and classification of regulated information

The common list of types of regulated information shall correspond to the types of information listed in the table of the Annex to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1338 and which relate to Directive 2004/109/EC.

## Article 7 — Repeal

Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1437 is repealed.

References to the repealed Regulation shall be construed as references to this Regulation.

## Article 8 — Entry into force and application

This Regulation shall enter into force on the third day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

It shall apply from 10 July 2026.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 4 May 2026.
