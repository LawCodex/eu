# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/912 of 27 April 2026 amending Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/891 as regards the Member States’ notifications concerning producer prices of fruit and vegetables on the internal market

**COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) 2026/912**

**of 27 April 2026**

**amending Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/891 as regards the Member States’ notifications concerning producer prices of fruit and vegetables on the internal market**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007 (1), and in particular Article 223(2) thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Regulation (EU) 2026/687 of the European Parliament and of the Council (2) implementing the bilateral safeguard clauses of the EU-Mercosur Partnership Agreement and the EU-Mercosur Interim Trade Agreement for agricultural products will be applied as from 1 May 2026. Article 4 of that Regulation requires the Commission to monitor some sensitive products on the Union market, in particular as regards import and export trends, production and price developments.

(2) The Annex to Regulation (EU) 2026/687 lists garlic among the sensitive products. In order to monitor price developments on the Union market, the Commission needs a regular availability of recorded prices for garlic.

(3) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/891 (3) requires Member States to notify the Commission of producer prices recorded on the internal market for the fruit and vegetables listed in Annex VI, which does not include garlic.

(4) To comply with the monitoring requirements set out in Regulation (EU) 2026/687, garlic should therefore be added to Annex VI of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/891.

(5) Considering that the bilateral safeguard clauses of the EU-Mercosur Partnership Agreement and the EU-Mercosur Interim Trade Agreement for agricultural products will be applied from 1 May 2026, this Delegated Regulation should enter into force the third day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

## Article 1

In Annex VI of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/891, the following row is added to the end of the table:

| ‘Garlic | All varieties | Size 45-60 mm packages of around 2-5 kg | Spain France Italy Romania Portugal Poland’ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the third day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 27 April 2026.

For the Commission

The President

Ursula VON DER LEYEN
