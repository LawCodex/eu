# Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1727 of 10 July 2026 amending Implementing Regulations (EU) 2020/761 and (EU) 2020/1988 as regards some provisions introduced by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/996

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2026/1727**

**of 10 July 2026**

**amending Implementing Regulations (EU) 2020/761 and (EU) 2020/1988 as regards some provisions introduced by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/996**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 December 2013 establishing a common organisation of the markets in agricultural products and repealing Council Regulations (EEC) No 922/72, (EEC) No 234/79, (EC) No 1037/2001 and (EC) No 1234/2007 (1), and in particular Article 187 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/761 (2) lays down the rules for the management of import and export tariff quotas for agricultural products managed by a system of import and export licences and provides for specific rules for that management.

(2) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1988 (3) lays down the rules for the administration of import tariff quotas designed to be used following the chronological order of dates of acceptance of customs declarations (‘first come, first served’ principle).

(3) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/996 (4) introduced changes resulting from the Interim Agreement on Trade between the European Union, of the one part, and the Common Market of the South, the Argentine Republic, the Federative Republic of Brazil, the Republic of Paraguay and the Oriental Republic of Uruguay, of the other part (‘the ITA’) (5) as regards tariff rate quotas, to Implementing Regulations (EU) 2020/761 and (EU) 2020/1988.

(4) The ITA is provisionally applied from 1 May 2026. Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/996 therefore applies from 1 May 2026 as well.

(5) In the tables for tariff rate quotas with order numbers 09.4920 and 09.4925 in Annex VIII to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/761, proof of trade is required if the reference quantity requirement is suspended. The minimum quantity for proof of trade, in case it would be required, should therefore be set out.

(6) The ITA provides that the Union opens a tariff rate quota for pigmeat originating from Paraguay. That tariff rate quota was opened by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/996. The tariff rate quota with order number 09.4945 in Annex X to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/761 concerns pigmeat originating from Paraguay only. Therefore, in the tariff rate quota table for that quota, the origin ‘Paraguay’ is laid down correctly in the origin row. However, the row ‘Specific entries to be made on the licence application and on the licence’ contains a contradictory text: in the second and fourth sentences, the references to the origin ‘Mercosur’ were incorrect because the origin of the pigmeat should have been solely Paraguay and not all of the Mercosur States.

(7) To correctly implement the ITA, the specific entries to be made on the licence should therefore be clarified.

(8) It is appropriate to rectify the mistakes described in recital 6 retroactively, as they might lead to a confusion as regards the scope of the licence issued for the tariff rate quota with order number 09.4945. In fact, for that tariff rate quota, no licence applications were received in the licence application period in May 2026, with the result that no import licences were issued for that tariff rate quota for the first sub-period of 2026. This amendment to Annex X to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/761 should therefore apply retroactively from the same date as Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/996, which is 1 May 2026.

(9) In Article 4(4a) of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1988, it is necessary to specify that reference is made to Articles 72a to 72d of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/761.

(10) For several tariff rate quotas for products originating from Mercosur States, the quantity is expressed in weight equivalent in the ITA. However, a number of conversion coefficients needed for the management of these tariff rate quotas are not included in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/996, as they are not included in the ITA. The missing conversion coefficients for meat products to convert product weight to carcass weight equivalent should be introduced in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/761.

(11) Tariff rate quota with order number 09.0881 set out in Annex I to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1988 concerns honey and the tariff quota period is divided in sub-periods. The quantity imported under that tariff quota in the first sub-period 2026 exceeded the available quantity for this first sub-period by 152 040 kg. To ensure that the pro-rata quantity for 2026 granted under tariff rate quota with order number 09.0881 is not exceeded, the quantity imported in excess during the first sub-period should be deducted from the quantity for the last sub-period of 2026.

(12) Implementing Regulations (EU) 2020/761 and (EU) 2020/1988 should therefore be amended accordingly.

(13) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee for the Common Organisation of the Agricultural Markets,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

## Article 1

Annexes VIII, X and XVI to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/761 are amended in accordance with Annex I to this Regulation.

## Article 2

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1988 is amended as follows:

(1) in Article 4(4a), the second subparagraph is replaced by the following:

‘This document may be issued electronically in accordance with the ELAN1L-TCDOC data model. In this case, during the transitional period, Articles 72a to 72d of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/761 apply.’;

(2) Annex I is amended in accordance with Annex II to this Regulation.

## Article 3

This Regulation shall enter into force on the third day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Annex I, point 2, shall apply from 1 May 2026.

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.

Done at Brussels, 10 July 2026.
