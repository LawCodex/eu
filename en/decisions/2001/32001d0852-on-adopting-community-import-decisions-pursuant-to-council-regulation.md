## Article 1

The final decisions on the import of the chemical substances ethylene dichloride and ethylene oxide as set out on the Importing Country Response Forms in Annex I are adopted.

## Article 2

The Annex to Decision 2000/657/EC is amended in accordance with Annex II to this Decision.
