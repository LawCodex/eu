**COMMISSION DECISION (EU) 2023/2440**

**of 27 October 2023**

**on the Union-wide total quantity of allowances to be allocated in respect of aircraft operators under the EU Emissions Trading System for 2024**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

## Article 1

The total quantity of allowances to be allocated in respect of aircraft operators for 2024 referred to in Article 3c(5) of Directive 2003/87/EC amounts to 27 563 529 .

## Article 2

The quantity of free allocation which would have taken place in 2024 under the rules for free allocation in force prior to the amendments introduced by Directive (EU) 2023/958 amounts to 23 429 000 .

## Article 2a

Taking into account the aircraft operators having ceased activity until 31 December 2023, 1 988 951 allowances shall be transferred to the Innovation Fund established by Article 10a(8) of Directive 2003/87/EC.

## Article 3

This Decision shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.
