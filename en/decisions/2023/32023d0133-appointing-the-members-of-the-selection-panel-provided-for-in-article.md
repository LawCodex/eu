## Article 1

For a period of four years from 20 January 2023, the following persons are appointed as members of the selection panel provided for in Article 14(3) of Regulation (EU) 2017/1939:

Mr Jean-François BOHNERT

Mr Vítor Manuel DA SILVA CALDEIRA

Mr Peter FRANK

Ms Margreet Fröberg

Ms Ulrike HABERL-SCHWARZ

Ms María Ángeles GARRIDO LORENZO

Ms Saale LAOS

Mr Ján MAZÁK

Mr Dražen JELENIĆ

Mr Lorenzo SALAZAR

Ms Martine SOLOVIEFF

Ms Johanna HERVONEN.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.
