# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1638 of 14 August 2023 amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/75 setting out the list of Interreg programme areas to receive support from the European Regional Development Fund and external financing instruments of the Union, broken down by strand and Interreg programme under the European territorial cooperation goal (notifed under document C(2023) 5460)

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2023/1638**

**of 14 August 2023**

**amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/75 setting out the list of Interreg programme areas to receive support from the European Regional Development Fund and external financing instruments of the Union, broken down by strand and Interreg programme under the European territorial cooperation goal**

**(notifed under document C(2023) 5460)**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2021/1059 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 June 2021 on specific provisions for the European territorial cooperation goal (Interreg) supported by the European Regional Development Fund and external financing instruments (1), and in particular Article 8(1) thereof,

After consulting the Committee established by Article 115(1) of Regulation (EU) 2021/1060 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 June 2021 laying down common provisions on the European Regional Development Fund, the European Social Fund Plus, the Cohesion Fund, the Just Transition Fund and the European Maritime, Fisheries and Aquaculture Fund and financial rules for those and for the Asylum, Migration and Integration Fund, the Internal Security Fund and the Instrument for Financial Support for Border Management and Visa Policy (2),

Whereas:

(1) By Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/75 (3), the Commission set out the list of Interreg programme areas to receive support from the European Regional Development Fund and external financing instruments of the Union, broken down by strand and Interreg programme under the European territorial cooperation goal for the period 2021 to 2027.

(2) The unjustified and unprovoked military aggression by the Russian Federation against Ukraine and the ongoing armed conflict has fundamentally changed the security situation in Europe. In its conclusions of 24 February 2022, the European Council condemned the military aggression against Ukraine as well as the involvement of Belarus in that aggression.

(3) As a result, the Commission has suspended the preparation of Interreg programmes between the Union and the Russian Federation and Belarus respectively, and the Union has imposed a series of sanctions against the two countries. Given the continuation of the war of aggression against Ukraine, the affected Member States and the Commission agreed not to pursue the preparation of Interreg programmes with the Russian Federation and Belarus in the programming period 2021-2027. The initially envisaged programme areas should therefore be deleted from the list of Interreg programme areas to receive support. For the same reasons, the initially planned participation of the Russian Federation and of Belarus in two transnational programmes should be cancelled.

(4) In order to help NUTS 3 regions lying directly on the borders, heavily impacted by the Russian aggression and not participating in other cooperation programmes to address the additional challenges due to the war of aggression, the inclusion of these regions to existing Interreg programmes should be made possible. The geography of a limited number of programmes should therefore be extended to cover the affected border regions.

(5) Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/75 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/75 is amended as follows:

1\. Annex I is replaced by Annex I to this Decision;

2\. Annex II is replaced by Annex II to this Decision.

## Article 2

This Decision is addressed to the Member States.

Done at Brussels, 14 August 2023.
