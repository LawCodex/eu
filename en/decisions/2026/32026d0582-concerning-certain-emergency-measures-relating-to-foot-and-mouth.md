## Article 1

Cyprus shall establish restricted zones, which comprise protection and surveillance zones, and a further restricted zone, in accordance with Article 21 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687, where the measures to be applied in such zones in accordance with that Delegated Regulation are applied at least until the dates listed in the Annex to this Decision.

Those protection, surveillance and further restricted zones shall comprise at least the areas listed in the Annex.

## Article 1a

Movements of animals of listed species, as set out in the Annex to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/1882, from the further restricted zone set out in the Annex to this Decision, to a destination located outside the external perimeters of that further restricted zone shall be prohibited until the dates listed in the Annex.

## Article 1b

A vaccination zone, as described in the Annex to this Decision, shall be established immediately by the competent authority, in accordance with Part 1 of Annex VII to Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/361, and under the conditions laid down in that Annex VII.

## Article 2

Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/484 is repealed.

## Article 3

This Decision is addressed to the Republic of Cyprus.
