# DECISION (EU) OF THE EUROPEAN CENTRAL BANK of 24 August 2026 on the identification of the Visa Europe Payment System as a systemically important payment system pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 on oversight requirements for systemically important payment systems (ECB/2026/22)

EN ECB-PUBLIC DECISION OF THE EUROPEAN CENTRAL BANK of 24 August 2026 on the identification of the Visa Europe Payment System as a systemically important payment system pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 on oversight requirements for systemically important payment systems (ECB/2026/22) THE GOVERNING COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN CENTRAL BANK,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Function ing of the European Union, and in particular Article 127(2) thereof,

Having regard to the Statute of the European System of Central Banks and of the European Central Bank, and in particular Article 3.1, Article 22 and Article 34.1 thereof,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 of the European Central Bank of 2 July 2025 on oversight requirements for systemically important payment systems (ECB/2025/22) 1, and in particular Article 3(1) and (3) thereof,

Whereas:

(1) The fourth indent of Article 127(2) of the Treaty and the fourth indent of Article 3.1 of the Statute of the European System of Central Banks and of the European Central Bank empower the Eurosystem to promote the smooth operation of payment systems.

(2) The Eurosystem promotes the smooth operation of payment systems, inter alia, by conducting oversight.

(3) To ensure the effective oversight of payment systems, t he European Central Bank (ECB) , through Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22), has implemented the principles for financial market infrastructures issued by the Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures (CPMI), previously known as the Committee on Payment and Settlement Systems (CPSS), and the Technical Committee of the International Organization of Securities Commission (IOSCO) . Those principles harmonise and strengthen existing international oversight standards for, inter alia, systemically important payment systems . The provisions of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22) also reflect the experience and findings of the oversight assessments conducted on the basis of 1 OJ L, 2025/1355, ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/1355/oj.

2 ECB-PUBLIC Regulation (EU) No 795/2014 of the European Central Bank (ECB/2014/28) 2 in the past years, as well as recent technological and regulatory developments in the Union.

(4) Pursuant to Article 3(1) of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22), the Governing Council identifies a payment system as a systemically important payment system (SIPS) if: it is eligible to be notified as a system pursuant to Directive 98/26/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council 3 by a Member State whose currency is the euro or its operator is established in the euro area, including establishment by means of a branch, through which the system is operated; and at least two of the criteria set out under Article 3(1), point (b), of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22) occur over a calendar year.

(5) Pursuant to Article 3(3) of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22), the Governing Council identifies the payment systems that are subject to that Regulation, their respective operators and c ompetent authorities. The identified SIPS operator s are accountable vis -à-vis the competent authorities for the compliance of the SIPS with the oversight requirements under Regulation (EU) 2025 /1355 (ECB/2025/22).

( 6) A SIPS operator should in principle be a legal entity in the euro area that is responsible for operating a SIPS. Exceptionally, however, the Governing Council may, on a case-by-case basis, also identify as a SIPS operator a branch that is established in the euro area and that is a legally dependent part of a legal entity established outside the euro area.

(7) In August 2023, VISA EUROPE Limited established a branch in Germany named VISA EUROPE Limited, German Branch 4 .

( 8) Based on the identification exercise carried out in 2025 5 , the Governing Council has verified that Visa Europe Payment System meets the criteria necessary for its identification as a SIPS, those being the criterion set out in Article 3(1), point (a), of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22) and t wo of the criteria set out in Article 3(1), point (b)(ii) and (iii), of that Regulation, as shown in the Annex to this Decision.

( 9) The Governing Council has identified VISA EUROPE Limited, German Branch as the operator of the Visa Europe Payment System in the euro area and, as such, it is the entity responsible for compliance of the Visa Europe Payment System with Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22).

(10) The ECB has sent a written notice to the addressee of this Decision, VISA EUROPE Limited, German Branch, of its intention to initiate a process to identify the Visa Europe P ayment System as a SIPS, s tating all material facts and legal grounds regarding that possible identification, in accordance wit h 2 Regulation (EU) No 795/2014 of the European Central Bank of 3 July 2014 on oversight requirements for systemically important payment systems (ECB/2014/28) (OJ L 217, 23.7.2014, p. 16, ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/795/oj) (repealed by Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22)).

(12) In view of the pan-European importance of the Visa Europe Payment System, and in the absence of a proven, long- standing oversight relationship between the Visa Europe Payment System and a Eurosystem national central bank over the previous five years, the ECB is the competent authority in line with Article 2, point (7)(b)(ii), of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22),

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Definitions Terms used in this Decision have the same meaning as those in Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22).

## Article 2

Identification of SIPS and SIPS operator

1\. The Visa Europe Payment System shall be identified as a systemically important payment system for the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22) in accordance with Article 3(1), point (a), and the criteria under 3(1), point (b)(ii) and (iii), of that Regulation and on the basis of the Annex to this Decision.

2\. In its capacity as operator of the Visa Europe Payment System for the euro area, VISA EUROPE Limited, German Branch, in accordance with Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22), shall ensure that this systemically important payment system complies with the requirements set out in Articles 8 to 27 and Article 29 of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22).

## Article 3

Competent authority The European Central Bank (ECB) shall be the competent authorit y for the oversight of the Visa Europe Payment System.

## Article 4

Taking effect 4 ECB-PUBLIC This Decision shall take effect on the day of its notification to the addressee.

## Article 5

Addressee This Decision is addressed to VISA EUROPE Limited, German Branch, Neue Mainzer Str. 66-68, 60311 Frankfurt am Main, Germany, in its capacity as systemically important payment system operator of the Visa Europe Payment System.
