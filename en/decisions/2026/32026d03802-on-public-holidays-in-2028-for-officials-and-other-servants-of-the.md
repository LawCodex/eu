# Commission Decision of 10 July 2026 on public holidays in 2028 for officials and other servants of the European Union serving in Brussels and Luxembourg

**COMMISSION DECISION**

**of 10 July 2026**

**on public holidays in 2028 for officials and other servants of the European Union serving in Brussels and Luxembourg**

(C/2026/3802)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to the Staff Regulations of Officials of the European Union and the Conditions of Employment of Other Servants of the European Union (CEOS), laid down by Council Regulation (EEC, Euratom, ECSC) No 259/68 (1), and in particular Article 61 of the Staff Regulations and Articles 16 and 91 of the CEOS,

Having regard to the joint rules establishing the list of public holidays of officials of the European Communities (2), and in particular Article 1(3) thereof,

Having regard to the opinion of the Staff Committee,

Having regard to the opinion of the Staff Regulations Committee,

Having regard to the opinion of the Heads of Administration,

Whereas:

(1) Pursuant to Article 61 of the Staff Regulations of Officials of the European Union and Articles 16 and 91 of the CEOS, the list of public holidays in 2028 for officials and other servants of the European Union serving in Brussels and Luxembourg should be adopted.

(2) In 2028, Easter Sunday falls on 16 April.

(3) In 2028, 24 December falls on a Sunday.

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:

## Sole Article

The public holidays in 2028 for staff serving in Brussels and Luxembourg are as follows:

| 13 April | Maundy Thursday |
| --- | --- |
| 14 April | Good Friday |
| 17 April | Easter Monday |
| 1 May | Labour Day |
| 9 May | Anniversary of the Declaration by Robert Schuman in 1950 |
| 25 May | Ascension Day |
| 26 May | Day after Ascension Day |
| 5 June | Whit Monday |
| 23 June* | Luxembourg National Holiday *for staff serving in Luxembourg |
| 21 July* | Belgian National Holiday *for staff serving in Brussels |
| 15 August | Assumption |
| 1 November | All Saints’ Day |
| 2 November | All Souls’ Day |
| 25 December to 29 December | 5 end-of-year days |
| TOTAL 2028: | 17 days |

Done at Brussels, 10 July 2026.

For the Commission

Piotr SERAFIN

Member of the Commission
