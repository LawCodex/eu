# Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/1882 of 30 July 2026 reviewing the list of persons, groups and entities covered by Decision (CFSP) 2026/455 on restrictive measures to combat terrorism

**COUNCIL DECISION (CFSP) 2026/1882**

**of 30 July 2026**

**reviewing the list of persons, groups and entities covered by Decision (CFSP) 2026/455 on restrictive measures to combat terrorism**

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 29 thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

(1) On 26 February 2026, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2026/455 (1). That Decision reproduces the substance of Articles 1, 2, 3 and 3a of Common Position 2001/931/CFSP (2) which will continue to be applied in accordance with the case-law of the Court of Justice of the European Union.

(2) Following a review by the Council, an updated list of natural or legal persons, groups or entities that commit, or attempt to commit, terrorist acts or which participate in, or facilitate, the commission of terrorist acts was included in Annex I to Decision (CFSP) 2026/455.

(3) In accordance with Article 10(1) of Decision (CFSP) 2026/455, it is necessary to review at regular intervals the names of natural or legal persons, groups or entities on the list to ensure that there are grounds for keeping them thereon.

(4) This Decision sets out the result of the review that the Council has carried out in respect of natural or legal persons, groups or entities set out in Annex I to Decision (CFSP) 2026/455.

(5) The Council has verified that competent authorities, as referred to in Article 2(2) and (3) of Decision (CFSP) 2026/455, have taken decisions with regard to all of the natural or legal persons, groups or entities on the list to the effect that they have been involved in terrorist acts as defined in Article 1(1) thereof.

(6) Following a review of the names of the natural or legal persons, groups or entities listed in Annex I to Decision (CFSP) 2026/455, the Council has also concluded that the measures set out in Article 2(1) and (5) of that Decision should continue to apply to those natural or legal persons, groups or entities.

(7) It is therefore necessary to keep in Annex I to Decision (CFSP) 2026/455 the names of the natural or legal persons, groups or entities listed therein,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

The list of natural or legal persons, groups or entities in Annex I to Decision (CFSP) 2026/455 is set out in the Annex to this Decision.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Done at Brussels, 30 July 2026.

For the Council

The President

T. BYRNE
