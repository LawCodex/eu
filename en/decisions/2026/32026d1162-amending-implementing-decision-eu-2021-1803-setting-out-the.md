# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1162 of 21 May 2026 amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/1803 setting out the provisional amounts allocated to each Member State from the resources of the Brexit Adjustment Reserve and the minimum amount of support to local and regional coastal communities (notified under document C(2026) 1768)

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/1162**

**of 21 May 2026**

**amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/1803 setting out the provisional amounts allocated to each Member State from the resources of the Brexit Adjustment Reserve and the minimum amount of support to local and regional coastal communities**

**(notified under document C(2026) 1768)**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2021/1755 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 6 October 2021 establishing the Brexit Adjustment Reserve (1), and in particular Article 4(5) thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/1803 (2), as amended by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/837 (3), the Commission set out the provisional amounts allocated to each Member State from the resources of the Brexit Adjustment Reserve (the ‘Reserve’) in accordance with the methodology laid down in Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2021/1755, as well as the minimum amount of resources to be spent in accordance with Article 4(4) of that Regulation.

(2) Through Regulation (EU) 2023/435 of the European Parliament and of the Council (4), Member States were allowed to transfer all or part of the provisional amount allocated to them under the Reserve to the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF). In 2023, twenty-three Member States have submitted a reasoned request to transfer a total amount of EUR 2 089 446 003 from the Reserve to the RRF. The Commission assessed those requests positively.

(3) In the context of the review of the Multiannual Financial Framework, Council Regulation (EU, Euratom) 2020/2093 (5) was amended and the maximum resources laid down in that Regulation for the Brexit Adjustment Reserve were reduced by the unused balance of the Reserve (initially planned for 2025 instalment). This outstanding amount available corresponds to EUR 584 264 090.

(4) Regulation (EU) 2021/1755 has been amended taking into account that reduction. Those changes in the maximum resources of the Brexit Adjustment Reserve should be reflected in the provisional amounts allocated to each Member State and the minimum amount of support to local and regional coastal communities.

(5) For reasons of programming, global provisional amounts allocated to each Member State are given in current prices.

(6) Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/1803 should therefore be amended accordingly.

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

In Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/1803, the Annex is replaced by the text set out in the Annex to this Decision.

## Article 2

This Decision is addressed to the Member States.

Done at Brussels, 21 May 2026.

For the Commission

Raffaele FITTO

Executive Vice-President
