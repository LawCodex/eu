# Council Decision of 22 September 2026 replacing an alternate member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for Estonia

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 22 September 2026**

**replacing an alternate member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for Estonia**

(C/2026/5005)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Decision of 22 July 2003 setting up an Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work (1), and in particular Article 3 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 5 March 2025 (2), the Council appointed full members and alternate members of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for the period from 6 March 2025 to 5 March 2028.

(2) A seat as alternate member in the government representatives category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Ms Elina SOOMETS.

(3) Estonia has submitted a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Mr Marko TALUR is appointed alternate member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work in place of Ms Elina SOOMETS for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 5 March 2028.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 22 September 2026.

For the Council

The President

T. BYRNE
