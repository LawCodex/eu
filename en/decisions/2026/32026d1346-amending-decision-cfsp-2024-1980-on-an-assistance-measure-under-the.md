# Council Decision (CFSP) 2026/1346 of 15 June 2026 amending Decision (CFSP) 2024/1980 on an assistance measure under the European Peace Facility to support the Beninese Armed Forces with military equipment designed to deliver lethal force

**COUNCIL DECISION (CFSP) 2026/1346**

**of 15 June 2026**

**amending Decision (CFSP) 2024/1980 on an assistance measure under the European Peace Facility to support the Beninese Armed Forces with military equipment designed to deliver lethal force**

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 28(1) and Article 41(2) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

(1) On 15 July 2024, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2024/1980 (1), which established an assistance measure under the European Peace Facility to support the Beninese Armed Forces with military equipment designed to deliver lethal force.

(2) The full implementation of the planned support to the Beninese Armed Forces will not be completed until after the end of the implementation period of Decision (CFSP) 2024/1980. That period should therefore be extended by six months from 15 July 2026 to 15 January 2027.

(3) Decision (CFSP) 2024/1980 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

In Article 1 of Decision (CFSP) 2024/1980, paragraph 4 is replaced by the following:

‘4. The duration of the assistance measure shall be 30 months from the date of adoption of this Decision.’.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 15 June 2026.

For the Council

The President

K. KALLAS
