# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1717 of 20 July 2026 on the harmonised standards for gas water heaters drafted in support of Regulation (EU) No 814/2013

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/1717**

**of 20 July 2026**

**on the harmonised standards for gas water heaters drafted in support of Regulation (EU) No 814/2013**

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/1717**

**of 20 July 2026**

**on the harmonised standards for gas water heaters drafted in support of Regulation (EU) No 814/2013**

## Article 1

The references of harmonised standards for water heaters drafted in support of Regulation (EU) No 814/2013 listed in the Annex to this Decision, shall be published in the Official Journal of the European Union.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

## ANNEX

No Harmonised standards drafted in support of Regulation (EU) No 814/2013

1\. EN 13203-1:2025

Gas fired domestic appliances producing hot water - Part 1: Assessment of performance of hot water deliveries
