# Council Decision of 21 April 2026 replacing a full member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for Lithuania

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 21 April 2026**

**replacing a full member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for Lithuania**

(C/2026/2454)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Decision of 22 July 2003 setting up an Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work (1), and in particular Article 3 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 5 March 2025 (2), the Council appointed full members and alternate members of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for the period from 6 March 2025 to 5 March 2028.

(2) A seat as full member in the trade union representatives category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Mr Audrius GELŽINIS.

(3) Lithuania has submitted a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Ms Odeta LEŠINSKAITĖ is appointed full member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work in place of Mr Audrius GELŽINIS for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 5 March 2028.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 21 April 2026.

For the Council

The President

K. KALLAS
