# Council Decision of 22 May 2026 replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Cyprus

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 22 May 2026**

**replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Cyprus**

(C/2026/2903)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/128 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 January 2019 establishing a European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 337/75 (1), and in particular Article 4 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 28 March 2023 (2), the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training for the period from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2027.

(2) A seat as member in the government representatives category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Mr Yiannis MOUROUZIDES.

(3) The Government of Cyprus has designated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Mr Charalambos EFSTRATIOU is appointed member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training in place of Mr Yiannis MOUROUZIDES for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 March 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 22 May 2026.

For the Council

The President

M. DAMIANOS
