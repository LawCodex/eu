# Political and Security Committee Decision (CFSP) 2026/1886 of 16 July 2026 on the appointment of the head of the military command and support cell of the European Union Security and Defence Initiative in support of West African countries of the Gulf of Guinea, and repealing Decision (CFSP) 2026/58 (EUSDI/1/2026)

**POLITICAL AND SECURITY COMMITTEE DECISION (CFSP) 2026/1886**

**of 16 July 2026**

**on the appointment of the head of the military command and support cell of the European Union Security and Defence Initiative in support of West African countries of the Gulf of Guinea, and repealing Decision (CFSP) 2026/58 (EUSDI/1/2026)**

THE POLITICAL AND SECURITY COMMITTEE,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 38 thereof,

Having regard to Council Decision (CFSP) 2023/1599 of 3 August 2023 on a European Union Security and Defence Initiative in support of West African countries of the Gulf of Guinea (1), and in particular Article 3 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Pursuant to Article 3(1) of Decision (CFSP) 2023/1599, the Political and Security Committee (PSC) is to exercise the political control and strategic direction of the European Union Security and Defence Initiative in support of West African countries of the Gulf of Guinea (the ‘Initiative’). Furthermore, the Council authorised the PSC to take relevant decisions in accordance with Article 38 of the Treaty, including decisions on the appointment of the head of the military command and support cell.

(2) On 16 December 2025, the PSC adopted Decision (CFSP) 2026/58 (2) appointing Colonel Emilio Asensio BAUTISTA MARTÍNEZ as head of the military command and support cell of the Initiative as from 9 January 2026.

(3) On 8 June 2026, the Dutch military authorities proposed the appointment of Captain André (Annius) VAN DER KAMP as head of the military command and support cell of the Initiative (the ‘proposed appointment’).

(4) On 16 June 2026, the Military Commander for the military pillar of the Initiative supported the proposed appointment.

(5) On 23 June 2026, the EU Military Committee supported the proposed appointment and recommended that the PSC approve it.

(6) A decision on the appointment of Captain André (Annius) VAN DER KAMP as head of the military command and support cell of the Initiative as from 27 July 2026 should be taken.

(7) Decision (CFSP) 2026/58 should be repealed,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Captain André (Annius) VAN DER KAMP is appointed as head of the military command and support cell of the European Union Security and Defence Initiative in support of West African countries of the Gulf of Guinea as from 27 July 2026.

## Article 2

Decision (CFSP) 2026/58 is hereby repealed.

## Article 3

This Decision shall enter into force on 27 July 2026.

Done at Brussels, 16 July 2026.

For the Political and Security Committee

The Chairperson

L. MANDEL VENSILD
