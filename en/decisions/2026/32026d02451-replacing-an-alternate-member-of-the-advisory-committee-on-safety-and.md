# Council Decision of 21 April 2026 replacing an alternate member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for the Netherlands

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 21 April 2026**

**replacing an alternate member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for the Netherlands**

(C/2026/2451)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Decision of 22 July 2003 setting up an Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work (1), and in particular Article 3 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 5 March 2025 (2), the Council appointed full members and alternate members of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for the period from 6 March 2025 to 5 March 2028.

(2) A seat as alternate member in the trade union representatives category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Mr Wim VAN VEELEN.

(3) The Netherlands has submitted a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Mr Simon Johannes VAN DER STOEL is appointed alternate member of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work in place of Mr Wim VAN VEELEN for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 5 March 2028.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 21 April 2026.

For the Council

The President

K. KALLAS
