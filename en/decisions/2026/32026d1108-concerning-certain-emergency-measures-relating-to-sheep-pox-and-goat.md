**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/1108**

**of 13 May 2026**

**concerning certain emergency measures relating to sheep pox and goat pox in Romania and repealing Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1015 and Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/2415**

**(notified under document C(2026) 3323)**

**(Only the Romanian text is authentic)**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

## Article 1

Romania shall establish restricted zones, which comprise protection zones, surveillance zones, and a further restricted zone, in accordance with Article 21 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687, and under the conditions laid down in that Article.

Those protection, surveillance, and further restricted zones shall comprise at least the areas listed in Parts A and B of Annex I to this Decision, and the measures required to be applied in such protection, surveillance, and further restricted zones shall be applied at least until the dates listed in that Annex.

## Article 1a

Romania shall prohibit, until the dates set out for the respective protection, surveillance and further restricted zones listed in Parts A and B of Annex I:

(a) movements of sheep and goats from the protection and surveillance zones to a destination located outside the external perimeter of a further restricted zone immediately surrounding those protection and surveillance zones; and

(b) movements of sheep and goats from a further restricted zone to any destination located outside the external perimeter of the further restricted zone.

## Article 1b

Romania shall apply the measures which are applicable to further restricted zones, as provided for in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687, in the areas listed in Annex II to this Decision until the dates set out therein.

Movements of sheep and goats from the areas listed in Annex II to this Decision to a destination located outside the external perimeter of those areas shall be prohibited until the dates set out in that Annex.

## Article 1c

Romania shall prohibit movements of caprine and ovine animals from the entire territory of Romania to a destination located in or in transit through another Member State or third country until 31 December 2026.

## Article 2

Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1015 and Implementing Decision 2025/2415 are repealed.

## Article 3

This Decision is addressed to Romania.
