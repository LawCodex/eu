**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/820**

**of 1 April 2026**

**concerning certain emergency measures relating to foot and mouth disease in Greece and repealing Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/732**

**(notified under document C(2026) 2350)**

**(Only the Greek text is authentic)**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

## Article 1

Greece shall establish restricted zones, which comprise protection and surveillance zones, and a further restricted zone, in accordance with Article 21 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687, where the measures required to be applied in such zones in accordance with that Delegated Regulation are applied at least until the dates listed in the Annex to this Decision.

Those protection and surveillance zones, and the further restricted zone, shall comprise at least the areas listed in the Annex.

## Article 2

Greece shall ensure that movements of animals of listed species for foot and mouth disease, as set out in the Annex to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/1882, from the further restricted zone, set out in the Annex to this Decision, to a destination located outside the external perimeter of that further restricted zone are prohibited until the dates listed in that Annex to this Decision.

## Article 3

Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/732 is repealed.

## Article 4

This Decision is addressed to the Hellenic Republic.
