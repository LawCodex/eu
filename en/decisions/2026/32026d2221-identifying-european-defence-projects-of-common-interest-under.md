# Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2221 of 28 September 2026 identifying European Defence Projects of Common Interest under Regulation (EU) 2025/2643 of the European Parliament and of the Council

**COUNCIL IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/2221**

**of 28 September 2026**

**identifying European Defence Projects of Common Interest under Regulation (EU) 2025/2643 of the European Parliament and of the Council**

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2025/2643 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 December 2025 establishing the European Defence Industry Programme and a framework of measures to ensure the timely availability and supply of defence products (‘EDIP Regulation’) (1), and in particular Article 35(3) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the European Commission,

Whereas:

(1) On 16 February 2026, the Commission launched a call for expressions of interest aimed at consulting the Member States on the identification of possible European Defence Projects of Common Interest (EDPCIs), within the meaning of Article 35 of Regulation (EU) 2025/2643. By the deadline of 29 May 2026, Member States submitted nine project proposals for possible EDPCIs.

(2) Pursuant to Article 35(5), point (b), of Regulation (EU) 2025/2643, the Commission invited the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy (the ‘High Representative’) and the European Defence Agency (EDA) to provide their expertise with a view to ensuring consistency with the priorities and objectives referred to in Article 35(2), points (b) to (d), of that Regulation, to complement the information provided by Member States regarding project proposals.

(3) Pursuant to Article 35(5), point (c), of Regulation (EU) 2025/2643, the Commission verified that all Member States and associated countries, and, where relevant, Ukraine, were informed of the emergence of a project and were given the opportunity to participate.

(4) The Commission, taking into account the information provided by the High Representative and the EDA, verified the compliance of the project proposals for possible EDPCIs under the call for expression of interest with the criteria laid down in Article 35(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/2643 and proposed to identify five EDPCIs.

(5) The identified EDPCIs reinforce the competitiveness of the European Defence Technological and Industrial Base throughout the Union, while contributing to the development of Member States’ military capabilities critical for the security and defence interests of the Union, and meet the criteria laid down in Article 35(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/2643.

(6) The Council examined the proposal for an implementing act and agreed to the identification of the proposed five EPDCIs with the understanding that the intent of the Commission is to regularly assess whether additional projects could be identified as EDPCIs and to launch a second call for expressions of interest before the end of April 2027 and, on that basis, to consider further proposals for the identification of EDPCIs.

(7) Regulation (EU) 2025/2643 provides for the Commission to be able, where relevant, to participate in the EDPCIs listed in the Annex to this Decision and allows participating Member States to decide to involve the High Representative and the EDA as observers of an EDPCI,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

The projects listed in the Annex are identified as European Defence Projects of Common Interest (EDPCIs), within the meaning of Article 35 of Regulation (EU) 2025/2643.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Done at Brussels, 28 September 2026.

For the Council

The President

K. KALLAS
