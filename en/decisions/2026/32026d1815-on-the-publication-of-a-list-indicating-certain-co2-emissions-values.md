# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1815 of 24 July 2026 on the publication of a list indicating certain CO2 emissions values per manufacturer as well as average specific CO2 emissions of all new heavy-duty vehicles registered in the Union pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2019/1242 of the European Parliament and of the Council for the reporting period of the year 2024 (notified under document C(2026) 5108)

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/1815**

**of 24 July 2026**

**on the publication of a list indicating certain CO2 emissions values per manufacturer as well as average specific CO2 emissions of all new heavy-duty vehicles registered in the Union pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2019/1242 of the European Parliament and of the Council for the reporting period of the year 2024**

**(notified under document C(2026) 5108)**

**(Only the Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Spanish and Swedish texts are authentic)**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/1242 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019 setting CO2 emission performance standards for new heavy-duty vehicles and amending Regulations (EC) No 595/2009 and (EU) 2018/956 of the European Parliament and the Council and Council Directive 96/53/EC (1), and in particular Article 11(1), first subparagraph, points (a), (b), (d) and (f), thereof,

Whereas:

(1) The average specific CO2 emissions of a manufacturer should be determined on the basis of data reported by Member States and manufacturers pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2019/1242 for vehicles of that manufacturer or transferred to that manufacturer.

(2) The zero- and low-emission factor for each manufacturer should be determined taking into account the zero- and low-emission heavy-duty vehicles reported.

(3) The CO2 emission reduction trajectory and the emission credits per manufacturer should be determined on the basis of the number of new heavy-duty vehicles reported.

(4) In accordance with the formula in point 2.7.1 of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2019/1242, applicable for the reporting period of the year 2024, the average specific CO2 emissions of all new heavy-duty vehicles registered in the Union should be determined on the basis of the data reported by all manufacturers for vehicles falling in the vehicle sub-groups 4-UD, 4-RD, 4-LH, 5-RD, 5-LH, 9-RD, 9-LH, 10-RD and 10-LH.

(5) Following its amendment by Regulation (EU) 2024/1610 of the European Parliament and of the Council (2), the scope of Regulation (EU) 2019/1242, as set out in Article 2 thereof, has been enlarged. Accordingly, from the reporting period of the year 2024 onwards, the share of new heavy-duty vehicles in a vehicle sub-group, in accordance with point 2.4 of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2019/1242, is to be calculated based on the number of heavy-duty vehicles of a manufacturer falling within the enlarged scope. Therefore, the values of the average specific CO2 emissions and of the CO2 emission reduction trajectory of a manufacturer, as well as the value of the average specific CO2 emissions of all new heavy-duty vehicles, set out in this Decision and its Annex, are no longer directly comparable to the values set out in the Decisions for the reporting periods of the previous years. The emission credits per manufacturer are unaffected by the change in the calculation of the heavy-duty vehicle sub-group shares and remain comparable.

(6) The values presented in this Decision and its Annex are based on the data available to the Commission on 2 July 2026,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1 — Average specific CO2 emissions per manufacturer

The average specific CO2 emissions per manufacturer, as referred to in Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2019/1242, in the reporting period of the year 2024, are indicated in the second column of the table of the Annex to this Decision.

## Article 2 — Zero- and low-emission factor per manufacturer

The zero- and low-emission factor per manufacturer, as referred to in Article 5(1) of Regulation (EU) 2019/1242, in the reporting period of the year 2024, is indicated in the third column of the table of the Annex to this Decision.

## Article 3 — CO2 emissions reduction trajectory and emissions credits per manufacturer

The CO2 emissions reduction trajectory and the emission credits per manufacturer, as referred to in Article 7 of Regulation (EU) 2019/1242, in the reporting period of the year 2024, are indicated in the fourth and fifth columns, respectively, of the table of the Annex to this Decision.

## Article 4 — Average specific CO2 emissions of all new heavy-duty vehicles

The average specific CO2 emissions of all new heavy-duty vehicles registered in the Union in the reporting period of the year 2024, as calculated by applying the formula in point 2.7.1 of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2019/1242 taking into account the new heavy-duty vehicles of all manufacturers, is 35,2 g/tkm.
