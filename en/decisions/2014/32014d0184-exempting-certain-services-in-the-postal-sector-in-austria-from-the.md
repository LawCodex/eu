**(Text with EEA relevance)**

**(2014/184/EU)**

## Article 1

Directive 2004/17/EC shall not apply to contracts awarded by contracting entities and intended to enable the following services to be carried out in Austria:

(a) management services for mailroom;

(b) value-added services linked with electronic media and provided entirely by such media;

(c) philatelic services;

(d) payment services provided on its own behalf;

(e) postal services for addressed international letters B2B and B2C.

## Article 2

This Decision is addressed to the Republic of Austria.
