**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 25 January 1999**

**concerning the external auditors of the national central banks**

**(1999/70/EC)**

## Article 1

1\. KPMG Bedrijfsrevisoren/KPMG Réviseurs d’Entreprises BV/SRL are hereby approved as the external auditors of the Nationale Bank van België/Banque Nationale de Belgique for the financial years 2023 to 2028.

2\. Baker Tilly GmbH & Co. KG Wirtschaftsprüfungsgesellschaft are hereby approved as the external auditors of the Deutsche Bundesbank for the financial years 2021 to 2027.

3\. KPMG Auditores S.L. is hereby approved as the external auditor of the Banco de España for the financial years 2023 to 2027.

4\. Mazars S.A. and Deloitte & Associés S.A.S. are hereby approved as the external auditors of the Banque de France for the financial years 2024 to 2029.

5\. Grant Thornton Ireland are hereby approved as the external auditors of Banc Ceannais na hÉireann/the Central Bank of Ireland for the financial years 2023 to 2027.

6\. Deloitte & Touche S.p.A. are hereby approved as the external auditors of the Banca d’Italia for the financial years 2023 to 2027.

7\. KPMG Audit S.à r.l. are hereby approved as the external auditors of the Banque centrale du Luxembourg for the financial years 2024 to 2028.

8\. EY Accountants B.V. are hereby approved as the external auditors of De Nederlandsche Bank for the financial years 2026 to 2032.

9\. BDO Austria GmbH Wirtschaftsprüfungs- und Steuerberatungsgesellschaft is hereby approved as the external auditor of the Oesterreichische Nationalbank for the financial years 2023 to 2027.

10\. PriceWaterhouseCoopers & Associados – Sociedade de Revisores Oficiais de Contas Lda are hereby approved as the external auditors of the Banco de Portugal for the financial years 2022 to 2026.

11\. PricewaterhouseCoopers Oy are hereby approved as the external auditors of Suomen Pankki for the financial years 2026 to 2031.

12\. Ernst & Young (Hellas) Certified Auditors Accountants S.A. are hereby approved as the external auditors of the Bank of Greece for the financial years 2022 to 2026.

13\. ERNST & YOUNG Revizija, poslovno svetovanje, d. o. o. are hereby approved as the external auditors of Banka Slovenije for the financial years 2027 to 2029.

14\. Baker Tilly Klitou and Partners Limited are hereby approved as the external auditors of the Central Bank of Cyprus for the financial years 2023 to 2027.

15\. Deloitte Audit Ltd are hereby approved as the external auditors of the Bank Ċentrali ta’ Malta/Central Bank of Malta for the financial years 2023 to 2027.

16\. Grant Thornton Audit, s.r.o is hereby approved as the external auditor of Národná banka Slovenska for the financial years 2024 to 2027.

17\. KPMG Baltics OÜ are hereby approved as the external auditors of Eesti Pank for the financial years 2026 to 2030.

18\. KPMG Baltics SIA are hereby approved as the external auditors of Latvijas Banka for the financial years 2025 to 2029.

19\. UAB ROSK Consulting are hereby approved as the external auditors of Lietuvos bankas for the financial years 2025 to 2027.

20\. Ernst & Young d.o.o. are hereby approved as the external auditors of Hrvatska narodna banka for the financial years 2025 to 2027.

21\. Ernst and Young Audit OOD are hereby approved as the external auditors of Българска народна банка (Bulgarian National Bank) for the financial year 2026.

## Article 2

This Decision shall be notified to the ECB.

## Article 3

This Decision shall be published in the Official Journal of the European Communities.
