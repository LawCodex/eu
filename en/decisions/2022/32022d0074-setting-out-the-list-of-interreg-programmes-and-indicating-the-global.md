## Article 1

The list of cross-border cooperation programmes, the global amount of the total support from the ERDF and, where applicable, the total support from each external financing instrument of the Union for each programme, is set out in Annex I.

## Article 2

The list of transnational cooperation programmes, the global amount of the total support from the ERDF and, where applicable, the total support from each external financing instrument of the Union for each programme, is set out in Annex II.

## Article 3

The list of interregional cooperation programmes, the global amount of the total support from the ERDF and, where applicable, the total support from each external financing instrument of the Union for each programme, is set out in Annex III.

## Article 4

The list of outermost regions’ cooperation programmes, the global amount of the total support from the ERDF and, where applicable, the total support from each external financing instrument of the Union for each programme, is set out in Annex IV.

## Article 5

The amounts transferred pursuant to Article 9(4) of Regulation (EU) 2021/1059, broken down by Member State, are set out in Annex V.

## Article 6

This Decision is addressed to the Member States.
