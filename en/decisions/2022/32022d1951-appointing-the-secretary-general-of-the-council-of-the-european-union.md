# Council Decision (EU) 2022/1951 of 13 October 2022 appointing the Secretary-General of the Council of the European Union for the period from 1 November 2022 to 31 October 2027

**COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2022/1951**

**of 13 October 2022**

**appointing the Secretary-General of the Council of the European Union for the period from 1 November 2022 to 31 October 2027**

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 240(2), first subparagraph, thereof,

Whereas the Secretary-General of the Council of the European Union should be appointed for the period from 1 November 2022 to 31 October 2027,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Ms Thérèse BLANCHET is hereby appointed Secretary-General of the Council of the European Union for the period from 1 November 2022 to 31 October 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall be notified to Ms Thérèse BLANCHET by the President of the Council.

It shall take effect on the date of its notification.

## Article 3

This Decision shall be published in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Done at Luxembourg, 13 October 2022.

For the Council

The President

P. BLAŽEK
