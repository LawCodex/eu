# Council Decision (CFSP) 2025/49 of 9 January 2025 amending Decision (CFSP) 2017/2074 concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Venezuela

**COUNCIL DECISION (CFSP) 2025/49**

**of 9 January 2025**

**amending Decision (CFSP) 2017/2074 concerning restrictive measures in view of the situation in Venezuela**

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 29 thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

(1) On 13 November 2017, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2017/2074 (1).

(2) On 4 August 2024, the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy (the ‘High Representative’) issued a declaration on behalf of the Union which stated that the Union continues to follow developments in Venezuela with great concern, including the fact that the presidential election held on 28 July 2024 in Venezuela did not meet international standards of electoral integrity. The declaration further stated that without the publication of the official voting records by the National Electoral Council of Venezuela (CNE), the officially published results of the election published on 2 August 2024 by the CNE cannot be recognised. The declaration also stated that the Union is seriously concerned about the growing number of arbitrary detentions and the continued harassment of the opposition, and called on Venezuelan authorities to put an end to arbitrary detentions, repression and violent rhetoric against members of the opposition and civil society, and to release all political prisoners.

(3) On 24 August 2024, the High Representative issued a second declaration on behalf of the Union on the post-election developments stating that the Union remains extremely concerned about the deepening political crisis in Venezuela. The declaration also stated that only complete and independently verifiable results will be accepted and recognised to ensure that the will of the Venezuelan people is respected, and that the Union will continue to facilitate a Venezuelan-led dialogue that provides guarantees to both sides and leads to the restoration of democracy and to solving the current humanitarian and socio-economic crisis in the country.

(4) In its conclusions of 17 October 2024, the European Council stated that it remained extremely concerned about the situation in Venezuela and the human rights violations reported following the 28 July 2024 presidential election. It urged the Venezuelan authorities to respect the democratic will of the Venezuelan people and to end violence, repression and harassment of the opposition and civil society. Furthermore, in those conclusions, the European Council stated that political prisoners must be released, and that only complete and independently verifiable results will be accepted and recognised. The European Council conclusions also stated that the Union will continue to work with regional partners to support the Venezuelan people in their democratic aspirations and that it stands ready to support a constructive and inclusive transition with guarantees for both sides, to resolve the country’s political impasse and restore democratic institutions peacefully. Moreover, the European Council stated in its conclusions that the Union is ready to mobilise all the instruments at its disposal to support democracy in Venezuela.

(5) In light of recent developments in Venezuela and the lack of progress towards a Venezuelan-led dialogue leading to the restoration of democracy and the rule of law, and in view of the continued grave human rights situation in the country, the Council considers that 15 persons should be included in the list of natural and legal persons, entities and bodies subject to restrictive measures set out in Annex I to Decision (CFSP) 2017/2074.

(6) Annex I to Decision (CFSP) 2017/2074 should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Annex I to Decision (CFSP) 2017/2074 is amended as set out in the Annex to this Decision.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Done at Brussels, 9 January 2025.

For the Council

The President

A. SZŁAPKA
