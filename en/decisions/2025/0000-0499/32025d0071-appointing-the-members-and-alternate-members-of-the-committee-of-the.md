**COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2025/71**

**appointing the members and alternate members of the Committee of the Regions for the period from 26 January 2025 to 25 January 2030**

## Article 1

The following are hereby appointed to the Committee of the Regions for the period from 26 January 2025 to 25 January 2030:

— as members, the persons listed by Member State in Annex I,

— as alternate members, the persons listed by Member State in Annex II.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.
