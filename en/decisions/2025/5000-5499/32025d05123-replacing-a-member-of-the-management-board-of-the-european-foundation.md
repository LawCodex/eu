# Council Decision of 16 September 2025 replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions for the Netherlands

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 16 September 2025**

**replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions for the Netherlands**

(C/2025/5123)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EU) 2019/127 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 January 2019 establishing the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions (Eurofound), and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 1365/75 (1), and in particular Article 4 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 28 March 2023 (2), the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions for the period from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2027.

(2) A seat as member in the representatives of employers’ organisations category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Ms Guusje DOLSMA.

(3) The employers’ organisation BusinessEurope has designated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Mr Jurre DE HAAN is appointed member of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions in place of Ms Guusje DOLSMA for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 March 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 16 September 2025.

For the Council

The President

M. BJERRE

(1) OJ L 30, 31.1.2019, p. 74.

(2) Council Decision of 28 March 2023 appointing members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions (Eurofound) (OJ C 116, 31.3.2023, p. 23).
