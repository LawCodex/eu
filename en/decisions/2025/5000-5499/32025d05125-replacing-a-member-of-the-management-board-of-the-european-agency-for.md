# Council Decision of 16 September 2025 replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work for Portugal

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 16 September 2025**

**replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work for Portugal**

(C/2025/5125)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EU) 2019/126 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 January 2019 establishing the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work (EU-OSHA), and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 2062/94 (1), and in particular Article 4 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 28 March 2023 (2), the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work for the period from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2027.

(2) A seat as member in the government representatives category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Mr Nelson FERREIRA.

(3) The government of Portugal has designated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Mr Carlos NUNES is appointed member of the Management Board of the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work in place of Mr Nelson FERREIRA for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 March 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 16 September 2025.

For the Council

The President

M. BJERRE

(1) OJ L 30, 31.1.2019, p. 58.

(2) Council Decision of 28 March 2023 appointing members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work (EU-OSHA) (OJ C 116, 31.3.2023, p. 19).
