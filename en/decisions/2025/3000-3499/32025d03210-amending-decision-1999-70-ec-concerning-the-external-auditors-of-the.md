# Council Decision of 27 May 2025 amending Decision 1999/70/EC concerning the external auditors of the national central banks, as regards the external auditors of Lietuvos bankas

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 27 May 2025**

**amending Decision 1999/70/EC concerning the external auditors of the national central banks, as regards the external auditors of Lietuvos bankas**

(C/2025/3210)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to Protocol No 4 on the Statute of the European System of Central Banks and of the European Central Bank, annexed to the Treaty on European Union and the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 27.1 thereof,

Having regard to the Recommendation of the European Central Bank of 4 April 2025 to the Council of the European Union on the external auditors of Lietuvos bankas (ECB/2025/11) (1),

Whereas:

(1) The accounts of the European Central Bank (ECB) and of the national central banks of the Member States whose currency is the euro are to be audited by independent external auditors recommended by the Governing Council of the ECB and approved by the Council of the European Union.

(2) The mandate of the external auditors of Lietuvos bankas, UAB ROSK Consulting, ended following the audit for the financial year 2024. It is therefore necessary to appoint external auditors from the financial year 2025 onwards.

(3) Lietuvos bankas has selected UAB ROSK Consulting as its external auditors for the financial years 2025 to 2027.

(4) The Governing Council of the ECB has recommended that UAB ROSK Consulting be appointed as the external auditors of Lietuvos bankas for the financial years 2025 to 2027.

(5) Following the recommendation of the Governing Council of the ECB, Council Decision 1999/70/EC (2) should be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

In Article 1 of Decision 1999/70/EC, paragraph 19 is replaced by the following:

‘19. UAB ROSK Consulting are hereby approved as the external auditors of Lietuvos bankas for the financial years 2025 to 2027.’.

## Article 2

This Decision shall take effect on the date of its notification.

## Article 3

This Decision is addressed to the European Central Bank.

Done at Brussels, 27 May 2025.

For the Council

The President

A. SZŁAPKA
