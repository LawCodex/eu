# Council Decision of 19 June 2025 appointing full members and alternate members of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for Greece

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 19 June 2025**

**appointing full members and alternate members of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for Greece**

(C/2025/3470)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Decision of 22 July 2003 setting up an Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work (1), and in particular Article 3 thereof,

Having regard to the list of candidates submitted to the Council by Greece,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 5 March 2025 (2), the Council appointed full members and alternate members of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for the period from 6 March 2025 to 5 March 2028.

(2) Greece has submitted a list of candidates for several posts to be filled,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

The following are appointed full members and alternate members of the Advisory Committee on Safety and Health at Work for the period ending on 5 March 2028:

I. REPRESENTATIVES OF NATIONAL GOVERNMENTS

| Member State | Full member | Alternate members |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greece | Ms Vasiliki PAPANASTASIOU | Ms Angeliki MOIROU Mr Ioannis KONSTANTAKOPOULOS |

II. REPRESENTATIVES OF TRADE UNIONS

| Member State | Full member | Alternate members |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greece | Mr Andreas STOIMENIDIS | Mr Ioannis GKOUTZAMANIS Mr Markos KONTIZAS |

III. REPRESENTATIVES OF EMPLOYERS’ ORGANISATIONS

| Member State | Full member | Alternate members |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greece | Ms Katerina DASKALAKI | Ms Stella TSIAMI Mr Yanis STAVROU |

## Article 2

The Council shall appoint the full members and alternate members not yet nominated at a later date.

## Article 3

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 19 June 2025.

For the Council

The President

A. DZIEMIANOWICZ-BĄK
