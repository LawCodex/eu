# Commission Decision of 25 February 2025 correcting Decision C(2024) 6658 of 30 September 2024 approving on behalf of the European Union, the position to be taken as regards amendments to Annexes 10-A and 10-B to the Free Trade Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Singapore

**COMMISSION DECISION**

**of 25 February 2025**

**correcting Decision C(2024) 6658 of 30 September 2024 approving on behalf of the European Union, the position to be taken as regards amendments to Annexes 10-A and 10-B to the Free Trade Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Singapore**

(C/2025/1361)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Decision (EU) 2019/1875 of 8 November 2019 on the conclusion of the Free Trade Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Singapore (1), and in particular Article 3 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Commission Decision of 30 September 2024 approving on behalf of the European Union, the position to be taken as regards amendments to Annexes 10-A and 10-B to the Free Trade Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Singapore (‘Agreement’) was published in the Official Journal of the European Union on 1 October 2024 (2).

(2) The Annex to that Decision contains errors. In the sixth recital of the draft decision of the EU-Singapore Trade Committee a reference to Article 10.18 of the Agreement was inserted, which is not relevant. In point 149 ‘Pálinka’ of Annex 10-B a footnote is missing. There are several errors and mistakes in the names of the listed geographical indications.

(3) Decision C(2024) 6658 should therefore be corrected accordingly,

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:

## Article 1

The Annex to Decision C(2024) 6658 is replaced by the Annex to this Decision.

## Article 2

This Decision shall be published in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Done at Brussels, 25 February 2025.

For the Commission

Christophe HANSEN

Member of the Commission

## ANNEX — DECISION No …/2025 OF THE EU-SINGAPORE TRADE COMMITTEE — of XX/YY 2025 — amending Annexes 10-A and 10-B to the Free Trade Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Singapore

THE TRADE COMMITTEE,

Having regard to the Free Trade Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Singapore, and in particular Articles 10.17.3 and 10.18 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) The Free Trade Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Singapore (‘the Agreement’) entered into force on 21 November 2019.

(2) Article 10.17.3 of the Agreement provides that the Trade Committee adopts a decision regarding the listing in Annex 10-B (Protected Geographical Indications) to the Agreement of the names from Annex 10-A (List of names to be applied for protection as Geographical Indications in the territory of the Parties) to the Agreement as soon as practicable after the procedures for protection of geographical indications have been concluded.

(3) Article 10.18 of the Agreement provides that the Parties agree on the possibility of amending the list of geographical indications for wines, spirits, agricultural products and foodstuffs listed in Annex 10-B to be protected by each Party.

(4) The Republic of Singapore has completed the procedure for protection in its territory of four names (“Bayerisches Bier”, “Guijuelo”, “Siurana”, “Utiel-Requena”) that were listed in Annex 10-A to the Agreement and were applied for protection as geographical indications of the Union.

(5) The Republic of Singapore has completed the procedure for protection in its territory of twenty-one names (“Amarone della Valpolicella”, “Bordeaux supérieur”, “Cariñena”, “Cassis de Dijon”, “Côtes de Bordeaux”, “Crémant de Bourgogne”, “delle Venezie / Beneških okolišev”, “Dresdner Christstollen / Dresdner Stollen / Dresdner Weihnachtsstollen”, “Finocchiona”, “Χαλλούμι / Halloumi / Hellim”, “Idiazabal”, “Καλαμάτα / Kalamata”, “Kölsch”, “Los Pedroches”, “Méditerranée”, “Mont d'Or / Vacherin du Haut-Doubs”, “Monti Iblei”, “Morbier”, “Prosecco”, “Speck Alto Adige / Südtiroler Markenspeck / Südtiroler Speck”, “Toscano”), that were not listed in Annex 10-A to the Agreement and were applied for protection as geographical indications of the Union.

(6) Following the changes of names of “Alsace” into “Alsace / Vin d’Alsace” and “Jamón de Teruel” into “Jamón de Teruel / Paleta de Teruel” in the Union’s territory, these two names should also be changed in Annex 10-A to the Agreement.

(7) Consequently, Annexes 10-A and 10-B to the Agreement should be amended by listing those additional twenty-five names as protected geographical indications of the Union in Annex 10-B and removing four of those names from Annex 10-A. The two names “Alsace” and “Jamón de Teruel” should also be changed in Annex 10-A,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Annexes 10-A and 10-B to the Free Trade Agreement between the European Union and the Republic of Singapore are replaced by the text in the Annex to this Decision.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.
