# Commission Decision of 12 December 2024 instructing the Central Administrator of the Union Registry to enter corrections to the national allocation tables of Bulgaria, Germany, Spain, France, Italy and Luxembourg into the Union Registry

**COMMISSION DECISION**

**of 12 December 2024**

**instructing the Central Administrator of the Union Registry to enter corrections to the national allocation tables of Bulgaria, Germany, Spain, France, Italy and Luxembourg into the Union Registry**

(C/2025/1377)

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 October 2003 establishing a system for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading within the Union and amending Council Directive 96/61/EC (1),

Having regard to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1122 of 12 March 2019 supplementing Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the functioning of the Union Registry (2), and in particular Article 47(3), second subparagraph, thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Member States submitted to the Commission the list of installations in their territory pursuant to Article 11(1) of Directive 2003/87/EC.

(2) In accordance with Article 2 of Commission Decision (EU) 2021/355 (3), the Commission raised no objections to the lists of installations covered by Directive 2003/87/EC as submitted by Belgium, Bulgaria, Czechia, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Ireland, Greece, Spain, France, Croatia, Italy, Cyprus, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Hungary, the Netherlands, Austria, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Finland and Sweden, except as set out in Article 1 and Annexes of that Decision.

(3) Member States determined and notified the preliminary annual amounts of free allowances per installation using the revised benchmark values determined in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/447 (4), pursuant to Article 14(5) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/331 (5).

(4) In Article 1 of Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/927 (6), it was determined that no adjustment needed to be applied to the preliminary annual amounts of free allowances pursuant to Article 10a(5) of Directive 2003/87/EC, as demand did not exceed the maximum amount.

(5) Bulgaria notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 16 October 2024. The historical activity level of the heat sub-installation and the electricity exchangeability factor of the refinery products sub-installation were corrected for installation BG-112.

(6) Germany notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 23 October 2024. The electricity exchangeability factor of the EAF carbon steel sub-installation was corrected for installation DE-207565.

(7) Spain notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 19 November 2024. The fuel input as part of product benchmarks, the total attributed emissions and directly attributable emissions were corrected for installation ES-449.

(8) France notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 20 November 2024. The non-carbon leakage heat sub-installation of installation FR-23 was split into a non-carbon leakage heat sub-installation and a carbon leakage heat sub-installation. Total energy input from fuels and measurable heat produced were also corrected for installation FR-23.

(9) Italy notified changes to its national allocation table, by letters dated 24 September 2024, 26 September and 3 December 2024. The carbon leakage status of the heat sub-installation and fuel sub-installation were corrected for installation IT-205811. The district heating sub-installation of installation IT-1108 was split into a heat sub-installation and a district heating sub-installation. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installations were corrected for installation IT-1153. Fuel sub-installations were added to installation IT-1153. The historical activity level of the fuel sub-installation was corrected for installation IT-235. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation and fuel sub-installation were corrected for installation IT-201859.

(10) Luxembourg notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 5 November 2024. The historical activity level of the fuel sub-installation was corrected for installation LU-13.

(11) The notified national allocation tables are in conformity with Article 10c of Directive 2003/87/EC, Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/331 and Decision (EU) 2021/355.

(12) It is therefore appropriate to instruct the central administrator to enter those corrections into the Union Registry,

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:

## Sole Article

The central administrator of the Union Registry is hereby instructed to enter corrections to the national allocation tables of Bulgaria, Germany, Spain, France, Italy and Luxembourg with the final annual amounts for the transitional free allocation of emission allowances for the period from 2021 to 2025, as set out in the Annex, into the Union Registry.

Done at Brussels, 12 December 2024.

For the Commission

Wopke HOEKSTRA

Member of the Commission
