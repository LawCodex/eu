**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2025/1160**

**of 5 June 2025**

**concerning certain emergency measures relating to sheep pox and goat pox in Bulgaria and repealing Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/948**

**(notified under document C(2025) 3705)**

**(Only the Bulgarian text is authentic)**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

## Article 1

Bulgaria shall ensure that:

(a) a restricted zone, which comprises protection zones, surveillance zones, and further restricted zones, is established, in accordance with Article 21 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687, immediately by the competent authorities of Bulgaria;

(b) the protection, surveillance, and further restricted zones, referred to in point (a), comprise at least the areas listed in Parts A and B of Annex I;

(c) the measures required to be applied in the protection, surveillance, and further restricted zones, referred to in point (a), are applied at least until the dates listed in Parts A and B of Annex I.

## Article 2

(1) Bulgaria shall prohibit, until the dates set out for the respective protection, surveillance or further restricted zones in Annex I:

(a) movements of sheep and goats from the protection and surveillance zones to a destination located outside the external perimeter of a further restricted zone immediately surrounding those protection and surveillance zones; and

(b) movements of sheep and goats from a further restricted zone to any destination located outside the external perimeter of this further restricted zone.

(2) Bulgaria shall also prohibit, until the end of the time when protection zones must be established within the external perimeter of a further restricted zone, movements of sheep and goats from those parts of the territory of Bulgaria that are not listed as a protection, surveillance or further restricted zone in Annex I to a destination located within the external perimeter of such further restricted zone.

The competent authority of Bulgaria may exceptionally authorise movements of sheep and goats from those parts of the territory of Bulgaria that are not listed in Annex I to a slaughterhouse located within the external perimeter of a further restricted zone in Bulgaria, provided that:

(i) The animals are moved directly to the slaughterhouse, and for immediate slaughter; and that

(ii) The competent authority has carried out a risk assessment which indicates that the risks deriving from the authorisation are negligible.

(3) Bulgaria shall prohibit movements of caprine and ovine animals from any part of its territory to a destination located outside Bulgaria until 31 December 2026.

## Article 3

Bulgaria shall apply the measures which are applicable to further restricted zones, as provided for in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687, in the areas listed in Annex II to this Decision until the dates set out therein.

Movements of sheep and goats from the areas listed in Annex II to this Decision to a destination located outside the external perimeter of those areas shall be prohibited until the dates set out in that Annex.

Fresh meat and meat products obtained from sheep and goats slaughtered in a further restricted zone following an exceptional authorisation pursuant to the second paragraph of Article 2(2) may only be moved in and placed on the market within the territory of Bulgaria.

## Article 4

This Decision is addressed to the Republic of Bulgaria.
