**of 4 November 2025**

**concerning certain emergency measures relating to infection with lumpy skin disease virus in Spain and repealing Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/2076**

**(notified under document C(2025) 7515)**

**(Only the Spanish text is authentic)**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

## Article 1

Spain shall establish:

(a) a restricted zone, which comprises protection and surveillance zones, in accordance with Article 21 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687, and under the conditions laid down in that Article. The protection zones and the surveillance zones, respectively, shall comprise at least the areas listed as such in Annex I;

(b) vaccination zones I and II, in accordance with Part 1 of Annex IX to Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/361, and under the conditions laid down therein. These vaccination zones shall comprise at least the areas listed as such in Annex II.

(c) in addition, Spain shall provide for the measures required to be applied in the protection and the surveillance zones, as well as in the vaccination zones I and II, referred to in points (a) and (b) of this Article respectively, to be applied at least until the dates listed in the Annexes.

## Article 2

Spain shall prohibit movements of bovine animals from the protection and surveillance zones set out in Annex I to a destination located outside the external perimeter of those protection and surveillance zones or outside that Member State until the dates listed in that Annex.

## Article 3

Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/2076 is hereby repealed.

## Article 4

This Decision is addressed to the Kingdom of Spain.
