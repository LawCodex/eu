# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/2028 of 6 October 2025 amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/845 fixing the definitive allocation of Union aid to Member States for school fruit and vegetables and for school milk for the period from 1 August 2024 to 31 July 2025 (notified under document C(2025) 6609)

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2025/2028**

**of 6 October 2025**

**amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/845 fixing the definitive allocation of Union aid to Member States for school fruit and vegetables and for school milk for the period from 1 August 2024 to 31 July 2025**

**(notified under document C(2025) 6609)**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EU) No 1370/2013 of 16 December 2013 determining measures on fixing certain aids and refunds related to the common organisations of the markets in agricultural products (1), and in particular Article 5(6) thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Pursuant to Article 3 of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/39 (2), Member States wishing to participate in the Union aid scheme for the supply of fruit and vegetables, bananas and milk in educational establishments (‘the school scheme’) are to submit to the Commission by 31 January each year their request for that Union aid relating to the next school year and, if applicable, update the request for Union aid relating to the ongoing school year.

(2) Based on the information Member States notified by 31 January 2024, Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/845 (3) fixed the definitive allocation of Union aid for the school scheme among the Member States that submitted requests for the period from 1 August 2024 to 31 July 2025 (‘the school year concerned’).

(3) Member States are required to notify any transfers of definitive allocations made after January 31st of the school year to the Commission by August 31st of the same year pursuant to Article 6 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/39.

(4) France and Portugal submitted notifications of transfers between the definitive allocations for school fruit and vegetables and for school milk for the school year concerned.

(5) Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/845 should therefore be amended accordingly.

(6) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee for the Common Organisation of the Agricultural Markets,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Annex I to Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/845 is replaced by the text set out in the Annex to this Decision.

## Article 2

This Decision is addressed to the Member States.

Done at Brussels, 6 October 2025.

For the Commission

Christophe HANSEN

Member of the Commission
