# Council Decision of 14 April 2025 replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work for Spain

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 14 April 2025**

**replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work for Spain**

(C/2025/2435)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/126 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 January 2019 establishing the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work (EU-OSHA), and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 2062/94 (1), and in particular Article 4 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 28 March 2023 (2), the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work for the period from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2027.

(2) A seat as member in the representatives of employees’ organisations category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Ms Ana GARCÍA DE LA TORRE.

(3) The employees’ organisation ETUC has designated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Ms Patricia RUÍZ MARTÍNEZ is appointed member of the Management Board of the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work in place of Ms Ana GARCÍA DE LA TORRE for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 March 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 14 April 2025.

For the Council

The President

K. KALLAS

(1) OJ L 30, 31.1.2019, p. 58.

(2) Council Decision of 28 March 2023 appointing members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work (EU-OSHA) (OJ C 116, 31.3.2023, p. 19).
