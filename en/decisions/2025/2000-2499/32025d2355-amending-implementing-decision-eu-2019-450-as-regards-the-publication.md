# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/2355 of 13 November 2025 amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/450 as regards the publication of references of European Assessment Documents for balcony and terrace glazing systems without vertical frames and external thermal insulation composite systems with rendering system and other construction products

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2025/2355**

**of 13 November 2025**

**amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/450 as regards the publication of references of European Assessment Documents for balcony and terrace glazing systems without vertical frames and external thermal insulation composite systems with rendering system and other construction products**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 305/2011 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2011 laying down harmonised conditions for the marketing of construction products and repealing Council Directive 89/106/EEC (1), and in particular Article 22 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) In accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 305/2011, following requests by manufacturers for European Technical Assessments, the organisation of Technical Assessment Bodies has adopted 66 European Assessment Documents.

(2) The 66 European Assessment Documents relate to the following references for construction products:

— balcony and terrace glazing systems without vertical frames (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 020002-00-0404’);

— external thermal insulation composite systems with rendering system (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 040083-00-0404’);

— factory-made products of extruded, foamed polyethylene terephthalate for thermal insulation;

— veture kits – prefabricated units for external wall insulation and their fixing devices (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 040914-00-0404’);

— kits made of insulation boards with protective finishing for inverted roofs;

— pre-wall mounting kits for windows and doors;

— thermal insulation elements of fibre-reinforced polymer which form a thermal break between structural elements;

— load-bearing thermal insulation elements which form a thermal break between balconies and internal floors (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 050001-00-0301’);

— spherical and cylindrical bearings with special sliding material made of ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 050004-00-0301’);

— spherical and cylindrical bearings with special sliding material made of fluoropolymer (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 050009-00-0301’);

— pre-assembled drainage panels made of gabions and geotextile;

— kits composed by subframe and fixings for fastening claddings and external wall elements (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 090034-00-0404’);

— variant: kits for external wall claddings mechanically fixed – extension of scope;

— glass-ceramic panels for external cladding;

— full-bodied sintered stone slabs for use in claddings, floorings, pavings, stairs, rainscreens and curtain walling kits;

— kits for self-supporting and interconnected ventilated cavity walls;

— wood-based composite panel for facades;

— rigid seismic sway brace for fire sprinkler pipe work;

— flexible plug expansion joints for road bridges with flexible filling based on a synthetic polymer as binder (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 120011-00-0107’);

— wood and metal lighting columns;

— glued laminated veneer lumber;

— glulam strengthened by laminated veneer lumber, glued laminated veneer lumber made of finger-jointed laminations and block-glued glulam made thereof;

— special cements – carbonation hardening cements requiring a particular curing process;

— blended cements with high filler content, ground granulated blast-furnace slag or pozzolanic material or both, and clinker;

— kits for the strengthening of concrete elements by externally bonded and near-surface-mounted carbon fibre-reinforced polymer strips (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 160086-00-0301’);

— couplers for mechanical splices of reinforcing steel bars (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 160129-00-0301’);

— stud connectors for anchoring in reinforced concrete members as well as for connecting steel members with reinforced concrete members;

— compressed solid earth blocks with or without lime or cement and wall kits made of these blocks and natural hydraulic lime mortar;

— column shoes for structural connections of reinforced concrete columns;

— structural hollow sections of weathering steel;

— fiber ropes with end connectors;

— thin metal composite sheets (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 210046-00-1201’);

— variant: assessment of components for wood-based composite panels not produced by the manufacturers themselves;

— non-load-bearing polyvinyl chloride panels for internal and external walls;

— low-bitumen-mass shingles, laminated or not, with mineral or synthetic reinforcement (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 220020-00-0402’);

— fastening screws with welded or cold-formed set screws;

— fixing clips and sliding clips for double standing seam roof;

— variant: road marking products – extension of scope;

— variant: compound for bitumen and bituminous mixtures based on crumb rubber powder – extension of scope;

— stone wool elements used for large-scale water storage in civil works;

— adhesives for wall claddings;

— products for installation systems for supporting technical building equipment;

— variant: anchor channels under fatigue cyclic shear with different lower load level;

— variant: anchor channels for controlled impact screwdrivers;

— mechanical fasteners for use in concrete (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 330232-01-0601’);

— post–installed fasteners in concrete under fatigue cyclic loading (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 330250-00-0601’);

— variant: plastic anchors for redundant non-structural systems in concrete and masonry, and for fixing façade claddings through angle brackets under seismic actions in concrete and masonry;

— screw anchors for use in masonry;

— variant: cast-in anchor bolts under fatigue or seismic actions;

— high-strength structural bolting assemblies for hydraulically operated, friction-less and torsion-free tightening;

— anchor devices for fastening personal fall protection systems to timber substructures (superseding technical specification ‘EAD 331846-00-0603’);

— distance-fixing systems for use in concrete and masonry;

— kits for connection of precast concrete façade elements to concrete structures based on anchor channels;

— support fixing assemblies;

— point fastening kits for roof panels;

— anchor devices for fastening personal fall protection systems to steel or aluminium trapezoidal sheeting substructures;

— permanent insulating shuttering kits for whole buildings;

— load-bearing sandwich panels with foam or polymeric core and skins of structural cement bonded particle boards;

— earthquake-resistant kits composed of steel-concrete composite beams, columns and beam-column joints;

— variant: non-load-bearing permanent shuttering kits or systems based on hollow blocks or panels of insulating materials and sometimes concrete: hollow blocks resistant to seismic actions;

— fibre-reinforced polymer kits made of a pultruded carbon fibre laminate and an epoxy adhesive;

— fibre-reinforced polymer kits made of a carbon or glass fabric and an epoxy resin;

— structural steel composite wall panels for building systems;

— high load-bearing and low load-bearing single anchor earth retaining structures made of steel;

— fire-resistant reactive air transfer grilles;

— caps with intumescent inlay for recessed luminaires in fire-resistant suspended ceilings.

(3) The 66 European Assessment Documents satisfy the basic requirements for construction works set out in Annex I to Regulation (EU) No 305/2011. It is therefore appropriate to publish the references to those European Assessment Documents in the Official Journal of the European Union.

(4) The list of references to European Assessment Documents for construction products is published pursuant to Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/450 (2). For reasons of clarity, references to new European Assessment Documents should be added to that list.

(5) Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/450 should therefore be amended accordingly.

(6) In order to enable the use of the 66 European Assessment Documents as early as possible, so that the manufacturers already holding a European Technical Assessment can place their product on the market, this Decision should enter into force on the day of its publication,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

The Annex to Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/450 is amended in accordance with the Annex to this Decision.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Done at Brussels, 13 November 2025.

For the Commission

On behalf of the President

Kerstin JORNA

Director-General

Directorate-General for Internal Market, Industry, Entrepreneurship and SMEs
