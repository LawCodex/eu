# Council Decision of 30 September 2025 replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Institute for Gender Equality for Germany

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 30 September 2025**

**replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Institute for Gender Equality for Germany**

(C/2025/5510)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EC) No 1922/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 December 2006 on establishing a European Institute for Gender Equality (1), and in particular Article 10 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision (EU) 2025/2802 (2), the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Institute for Gender Equality for the period from 1 June 2025 to 31 May 2028.

(2) A seat as member of the Management Board of the European Institute for Gender Equality has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Ms Petra FOLLMER-OTTO.

(3) The Government of Germany has nominated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Ms Katharina JESTAEDT is appointed member of the Management Board of the European Institute for Gender Equality in place of Ms Petra FOLLMER-OTTO for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 May 2028.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 30 September 2025.

For the Council

The President

M. BØDSKOV
