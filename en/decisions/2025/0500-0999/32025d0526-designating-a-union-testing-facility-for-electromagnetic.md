# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/526 of 21 March 2025 designating a Union testing facility for electromagnetic compatibility in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 of the European Parliament and of the Council

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2025/526**

**of 21 March 2025**

**designating a Union testing facility for electromagnetic compatibility in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 of the European Parliament and of the Council**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 20 June 2019 on market surveillance and compliance of products and amending Directive 2004/42/EC and Regulations (EC) No 765/2008 and (EU) No 305/2011 (1), and in particular Article 21(2) thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Article 1(1) of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/1267 (2) provides that public testing facilities of Member States are to be designated as Union testing facilities following a call for expression of interest.

(2) The Commission launched the call for expression of interest CFEI/GRO/IMA/25/2711/14318 for three categories: construction products, electromagnetic compatibility, and pollutant emissions of non-road mobile machinery.

(3) The Communications Regulatory Authority of the Republic of Lithuania applied in the context of the call for expression of interest for the selection as Union testing facility for electromagnetic compatibility.

(4) An evaluation of the application made by Lithuania was carried out pursuant to the applicable rules and the conditions set out in the call for expression of interest.

(5) Based on the results of the evaluation, the Communications Regulatory Authority of the Republic of Lithuania should be designated as Union testing facility for electromagnetic compatibility.

(6) In accordance with Article 2 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/1267, the designation of a Union testing facility is to be reviewed on a regular basis and the time-limit for that review is to be set out in a decision to designate the testing facility,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1 — Union testing facility for the category of electromagnetic compatibility

The Communications Regulatory Authority of the Republic of Lithuania, Mortos str. 14, LT-03219 Vilnius, Lithuania is hereby designated as a Union testing facility for electromagnetic compatibility.

## Article 2 — Review of the designation

The designation as a Union testing facility referred to in Article 1 of this Decision shall be reviewed in accordance with Article 2 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/1267 by 14 April 2030, and every five years thereafter.

## Article 3 — Entry into force

This Decision shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Done at Brussels, 21 March 2025.

For the Commission

The President

Ursula VON DER LEYEN
