# Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/696 of 3 April 2025 amending the Annex to Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/672 concerning certain emergency measures relating to outbreaks of foot and mouth disease in Hungary and Slovakia (notified under document C(2025) 2169)

**COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2025/696**

**of 3 April 2025**

**amending the Annex to Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/672 concerning certain emergency measures relating to outbreaks of foot and mouth disease in Hungary and Slovakia**

**(notified under document C(2025) 2169)**

**(Only the German, Hungarian and Slovak texts are authentic)**

**(Text with EEA relevance)**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2016/429 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 9 March 2016 on transmissible animal diseases and amending and repealing certain acts in the area of animal health (‘Animal Health Law’) (1), and in particular Article 259(1), point (c), thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Foot and mouth disease is an infectious viral disease affecting animals in the mammal order of Artiodactyla and can have a severe impact on the concerned animal population and the profitability of farming causing disturbance to movements of consignments of those animals and products thereof within the Union and exports to third countries.

(2) In the event of an outbreak of foot and mouth disease in animals of listed species, there is a serious risk of the spread of that disease to other establishments keeping animals of listed species.

(3) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687 (2) supplements the rules for the control of the listed diseases referred to in Article 9(1), points (a), (b) and (c), of Regulation (EU) 2016/429, and defined as category A, B and C diseases in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/1882 (3). In particular, Articles 21 and 22 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687 provide for the establishment of a restricted zone in the event of an outbreak of a category A disease, including foot and mouth disease, and for certain measures to be applied therein. Article 21(1) of that Delegated Regulation provides that the restricted zone is to comprise a protection zone, a surveillance zone, and, if necessary, further restricted zones around or adjacent to the protection and surveillance zones.

(4) Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/672 (4) was adopted on the basis of Regulation (EU) 2016/429, and it lays down certain emergency measures, relating to outbreaks of foot and mouth disease in establishments keeping bovine animals in the County of Győr-Moson-Sopron in Hungary, close to the border with Austria, and in the region of Trnava in Slovakia, close to the border with Hungary. More particularly, Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/672 provides for the establishment of protection, surveillance and further restricted zones in Hungary and Slovakia, and a surveillance zone and further restricted zones in Austria, in accordance with Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687, following those outbreaks, and that those zones must comprise at least the areas listed in the Annex to that Implementing Decision. Due to the severity of the epidemiological situation, it also provides for certain restrictions on movements of animals of susceptible species.

(5) Since the adoption of Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/672, Slovakia and Hungary have informed the Commission of the confirmation of new outbreaks of foot and mouth disease in establishments keeping bovine animals. These new outbreaks are located in the Bratislava region in Slovakia, close to the border with Austria and in the County of Győr-Moson-Sopron in Hungary, close to the border with Slovakia. In accordance with Delegated Regulation (EU) 2020/687, Slovakia, Hungary and Austria, in cooperation together, have established new restricted zones, which comprise protection, surveillance zones and further restricted zones in Slovakia and Hungary, and a surveillance zone and a further restricted zone in Austria, where the disease control measures laid down in that Delegated Regulation are applied in order to prevent the further spread of that disease to other parts of those Member States, or to other Member States.

(6) Therefore, the areas included in those restricted zones should be defined, both spatially and temporally, in the Annex to Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/672, in order to prevent the further spread of the disease, as well as to prevent any unnecessary disturbance to trade within the Union, and to avoid any unjustified barriers to trade being imposed by third countries. Accordingly, regionalisation needs to be updated at Union level, and the areas identified as protection, surveillance and further restricted zones in Slovakia and Hungary, and as surveillance and further restricted zones in Austria, should be set out in the Annex to this Decision, and the duration of those measures fixed, in collaboration with those Member States.

(7) The Annex to Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/672 should therefore be amended accordingly.

(8) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Standing Committee on Plants, Animals, Food and Feed,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

The Annex of Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/672 is replaced by the text set out in the Annex to this Implementing Decision.

## Article 2

This Decision is addressed to Hungary, the Republic of Austria and the Slovak Republic.

Done at Brussels, 3 April 2025.

For the Commission
