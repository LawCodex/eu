### Article 1

The definitive allocation of Union aid for school fruit and vegetables and for school milk among Member States participating in the school scheme for the period from 1 August 2021 to 31 July 2022 is set out in Annex I.

### Article 2

Annex I to Implementing Decision (EU) 2020/467 is replaced by the text set out in Annex II to this Decision.

### Article 3

This Decision is addressed to the Member States.
