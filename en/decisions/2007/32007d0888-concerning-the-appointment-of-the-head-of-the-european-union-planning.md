**POLITICAL AND SECURITY COMMITTEE DECISION EUPT/2/2007**

**concerning the appointment of the Head of the European Union Planning Team (EUPT Kosovo)**

**(2007/888/CFSP)**

## Article 1

Roy REEVE is hereby appointed Head of the EU Planning Team (EUPT Kosovo) regarding a possible EU crisis management operation in the field of rule of law and possible other areas in Kosovo.

## Article 2

This Decision shall take effect on 1 January 2008.
