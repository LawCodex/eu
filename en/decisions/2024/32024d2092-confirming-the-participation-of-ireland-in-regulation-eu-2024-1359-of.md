# Commission Decision (EU) 2024/2092 of 31 July 2024 confirming the participation of Ireland in Regulation (EU) 2024/1359 of the European Parliament and of the Council addressing situations of crisis and force majeure in the field of migration and asylum

**COMMISSION DECISION (EU) 2024/2092**

**of 31 July 2024**

**confirming the participation of Ireland in Regulation (EU) 2024/1359 of the European Parliament and of the Council addressing situations of crisis and force majeure in the field of migration and asylum**

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

Having regard to Protocol No 21 on the position of the United Kingdom and Ireland in respect of the Area of Freedom, Security and Justice, annexed to the Treaty on European Union and to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 4 thereof,

Having regard to the notification by Ireland of its wish to accept and be bound by Regulation (EU) 2024/1359 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 May 2024 addressing situations of crisis and force majeure in the field of migration and asylum and amending Regulation (EU) 2021/1147 (1),

Whereas:

(1) Regulation (EU) 2024/1359 was adopted on 14 May 2024 and entered into force on 11 June 2024. Its provisions are to apply from 1 July 2026.

(2) By letter of 27 June 2024, Ireland notified its wish to accept and be bound by Regulation (EU) 2024/1359.

(3) The participation of Ireland in Regulation (EU) 2024/1359 should therefore be confirmed.

(4) There are no specific conditions attached to the participation of Ireland in Regulation (EU) 2024/1359 and no transitional measures are necessary,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

The participation of Ireland in Regulation (EU) 2024/1359 is confirmed.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Done at Brussels, 31 July 2024.

For the Commission

The President

Ursula VON DER LEYEN
