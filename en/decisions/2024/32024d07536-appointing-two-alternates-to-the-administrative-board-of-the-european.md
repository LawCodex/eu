# Council Decision of 16 December 2024 appointing two alternates to the Administrative Board of the European Union Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 16 December 2024**

**appointing two alternates to the Administrative Board of the European Union Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators**

(C/2024/7536)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/942 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 June 2019 establishing a European Union Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators (1), and in particular Article 18 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) Regulation (EU) 2019/942 provides that the Council is to appoint five members and their alternates to the Administrative Board of the European Union Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators (‘the Administrative Board’).

(2) Regulation (EU) 2019/942 provides that a member of the Administrative Board is not to be a member of the Board of Regulators of the European Union Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators and that the members of the Administrative Board are to act independently and objectively in the interest of the Union as a whole.

(3) On 8 December 2023, the Council adopted a Decision (2) appointing three members and three alternates to the Administrative Board for a period of four years from 28 January 2024. On 12 October 2024, one of those alternates, Ms Karin LUNNING, resigned as alternate of the Administrative Board.

(4) On 24 May 2024, the Council adopted a Decision (3) appointing two alternates to the Administrative Board for a period ending on 28 January 2026. On 27 September 2024, one of those alternates, Mr Wouter RAAB, resigned as alternate of the Administrative Board.

(5) The Council should appoint two alternates to replace Ms Karin LUNNING and Mr Wouter RAAB for the remainder of their respective terms of office,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

The following persons are hereby appointed as alternates to the Administrative Board:

— Ms Emmanuela TRULI, Greece, with a mandate ending on 28 January 2028,

— Mr Atanas GEORGIEV, Bulgaria, with a mandate ending on 28 January 2026.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 16 December 2024.

For the Council

The President

LANTOS C.
