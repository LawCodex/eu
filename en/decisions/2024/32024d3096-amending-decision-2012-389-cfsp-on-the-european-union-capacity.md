# Council Decision (CFSP) 2024/3096 of 5 December 2024 amending Decision 2012/389/CFSP on the European Union Capacity Building Mission in Somalia (EUCAP Somalia)

**COUNCIL DECISION (CFSP) 2024/3096**

**of 5 December 2024**

**amending Decision 2012/389/CFSP on the European Union Capacity Building Mission in Somalia (EUCAP Somalia)**

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 42(4) and Article 43(2) thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy,

Whereas:

(1) On 16 July 2012, the Council adopted Decision 2012/389/CFSP (1), which established a European Union Mission on Regional Maritime Capacity Building in the Horn of Africa (EUCAP NESTOR).

(2) On 12 December 2016, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2016/2240 (2), which amended the Mission’s mandate to focus it on capacity building in Somalia and changed its name to EUCAP Somalia.

(3) On 12 December 2022, the Council adopted Decision (CFSP) 2022/2445 (3), which amended Decision 2012/389/CFSP and extended EUCAP Somalia until 31 December 2024.

(4) In the context of the Holistic and Coordinated Strategic Review of CSDP engagement in Somalia and the Horn of Africa, the Political and Security Committee (PSC) agreed that EUCAP Somalia should be extended until 28 February 2027. Furthermore, the PSC agreed that EUCAP Somalia should contribute to advising, training and equipping the Federal Somali Police Force to contribute to the gradual establishment of the rule of law in Somalia, and, in addition, assist Somalia in strengthening its maritime security capacity in order to enable it to enforce maritime law more effectively.

(5) Decision 2012/389/CFSP should therefore be amended accordingly,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Decision 2012/389/CFSP is amended as follows:

(1) Articles 2 and 3 are replaced by the following:

## ‘Article 2 — Mission Statement

1\. EUCAP Somalia shall contribute to advising, training and equipping the Federal Somali Police Force to contribute to the gradual establishment of the rule of law in Somalia.

2\. In addition, EUCAP Somalia shall assist Somalia in strengthening its maritime security capacity in order to enable it to enforce maritime law more effectively.

## Article 3 — Tasks

1\. In order to achieve the Mission Statement set out in Article 2, EUCAP Somalia shall:

(a) advise, train, and equip the Federal Somali Police Force, in particular the Federal Darwish;

(b) advise the Ministry of Internal Security on the development and implementation of the necessary legal framework for the internal security architecture and the Federal Police of Somalia;

(c) advise Somali authorities on police functions and synergies between the land and maritime branches of the police and other security sector actors;

(d) provide monitoring to the Somaliland Maritime Police Force and to the Puntland Maritime Police Force;

(e) gradually transfer to EUTM Somalia, and hand over by 31 December 2025, the responsibility to advise the Somali Navy on coastguard functions, while continuing to make available maritime advisers on civilian and police related coast guard functions.

2\. In addition, upon invitation from the Republic of Djibouti, EUCAP Somalia shall, within its means and capabilities, provide training in the Djibouti Regional Training Centre, notably to support the capacity-building efforts of the Djibouti Coast Guard.’

;

(2) in Article 13(1), the following subparagraph is added:

‘The financial reference amount intended to cover the expenditure related to EUCAP Somalia for the period from 1 January 2025 to 28 February 2027 shall be EUR 110 240 125,06.’

;

(3) in Article 16, the second paragraph is replaced by the following:

‘It shall apply until 28 February 2027.’.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 5 December 2024.

For the Council

The President
