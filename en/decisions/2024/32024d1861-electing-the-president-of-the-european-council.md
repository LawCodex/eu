# European Council Decision (EU) 2024/1861 of 27 June 2024 electing the President of the European Council

**EUROPEAN COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2024/1861**

**of 27 June 2024**

**electing the President of the European Council**

**EUROPEAN COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2024/1861**

**of 27 June 2024**

**electing the President of the European Council**

## Article 1

Mr António COSTA is hereby elected President of the European Council for the period from 1 December 2024 until 31 May 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.
