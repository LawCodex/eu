# Council Decision of 12 December 2024 replacing an alternate member of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions for Denmark

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 12 December 2024**

**replacing an alternate member of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions for Denmark**

(C/2024/7463)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/127 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 January 2019 establishing the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions (Eurofound), and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 1365/75 (1), and in particular Article 4 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 28 March 2023 (2), the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions for the period from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2027.

(2) A seat as alternate member in the government representatives category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Ms Selma POURCHASSE-WINKLER.

(3) The Government of Denmark has designated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Ms. Christina LARSEN is appointed alternate member of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions in place of Ms Selma POURCHASSE-WINKLER for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 March 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 12 December 2024.

For the Council

The President

PINTÉR S.

(1) OJ L 30, 31.1.2019, p. 74.

(2) Council Decision of 28 March 2023 appointing members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions (Eurofound) (OJ C 116, 31.3.2023, p. 23).
