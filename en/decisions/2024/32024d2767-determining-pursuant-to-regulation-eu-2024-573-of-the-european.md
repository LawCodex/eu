For the purpose of the quota allocation for the placing on the Union market of hydrofluorocarbons, the reference values for each importer and producer referred to in point 4(a) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573 shall be those set out in Annex I to this Decision, and the reference values for each importer and producer referred to in point 4(b) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573 shall be those set out in Annex II to this Decision.
