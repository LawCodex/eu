# European Council Decision (EU) 2024/1862 of 27 June 2024 proposing to the European Parliament a candidate for President of the Commission

**EUROPEAN COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2024/1862**

**of 27 June 2024**

**proposing to the European Parliament a candidate for President of the Commission**

**EUROPEAN COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2024/1862**

**of 27 June 2024**

**proposing to the European Parliament a candidate for President of the Commission**

## Article 1

Ms Ursula VON DER LEYEN is hereby proposed to the European Parliament as candidate for President of the Commission.

## Article 2

This Decision shall be forwarded to the European Parliament.

## Article 3

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption. It shall be published in the Official Journal of the European Union.
