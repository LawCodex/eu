# Council Decision of 22 July 2024 replacing an alternate member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Greece

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 22 July 2024**

**replacing an alternate member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Greece**

(C/2024/4881)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/128 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 January 2019 establishing a European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 337/75 (1), and in particular Article 4 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 28 March 2023 (2), the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training for the period from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2027.

(2) A seat as alternate member in the representatives of employers’ organisations category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Mr Nikos GAVALAKIS.

(3) The employers’ organisation BusinessEurope has designated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Mr Kyriakos FILINIS is appointed alternate member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training in place of Mr Nikos GAVALAKIS for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 March 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 22 July 2024.

For the Council

The President

J. BORRELL FONTELLES

(1) OJ L 30, 31.1.2019, p. 90.

(2) Council Decision of 28 March 2023 appointing members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) (OJ C 116, 31.3.2023, p. 15).
