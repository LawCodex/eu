# Council Decision of 24 September 2024 replacing an alternate member of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions for the Czech Republic

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 24 September 2024**

**replacing an alternate member of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions for the Czech Republic**

(C/2024/5936)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/127 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 January 2019 establishing the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions (Eurofound), and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 1365/75 (1), and in particular Article 4 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 28 March 2023 (2), the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions for the period from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2027.

(2) A seat as alternate member in the government representatives category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Mr. Ondřej KRÝSL.

(3) The Government of the Czech Republic has designated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Mr. Tomáš HATAJ is appointed alternate member of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions in place of Mr. Ondřej KRÝSL for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 March 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 24 September 2024.

For the Council

The President

BÓKA J.

(1) OJ L 30, 31.1.2019, p. 74.

(2) Council Decision of 28 March 2023 appointing members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Foundation for the improvement of living and working conditions (Eurofound) (OJ C 116, 31.3.2023, p. 23).
