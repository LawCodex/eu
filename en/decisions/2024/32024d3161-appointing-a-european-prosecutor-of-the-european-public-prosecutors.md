**COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2024/3161**

**of 12 December 2024**

**appointing a European Prosecutor of the European Public Prosecutor’s Office**

## Article 1

The following candidate nominated by Poland is hereby appointed European Prosecutor of the EPPO for a non-renewable period of 6 years from 8 January 2025:

Ms Grażyna STRONIKOWSKA.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.
