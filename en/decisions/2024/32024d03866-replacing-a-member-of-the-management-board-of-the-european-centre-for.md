# Council Decision of 13 June 2024 replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Latvia

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 13 June 2024**

**replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Latvia**

(C/2024/3866)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/128 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 January 2019 establishing a European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 337/75 (1), and in particular Article 4 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 28 March 2023 (2), the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training for the period from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2027.

(2) A seat as member in the representatives of employers’ organisations category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Ms Ilona KIUKUCĀNE.

(3) The employers’ organisation BusinessEurope has designated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Ms Liene VOROŅENKO is appointed member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training in place of Ms Ilona KIUKUCĀNE for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 March 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 13 June 2024.

For the Council

The President

N. DE MOOR

(1) OJ L 30, 31.1.2019, p. 90.

(2) Council Decision of 28 March 2023 appointing members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) (OJ C 116, 31.3.2023, p. 15).
