# Council Decision (EU) 2024/2058 of 24 July 2024 on the non-replacement of Members of the Commission

**COUNCIL DECISION (EU) 2024/2058**

**of 24 July 2024**

**on the non-replacement of Members of the Commission**

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 246, third paragraph, thereof,

Having regard to the proposal from the President of the European Commission,

Whereas:

(1) Following the 2024 elections to the European Parliament, Commissioner VĂLEAN and Commissioner SINKEVIČIUS resigned as Members of the Commission with effect from 15 and 16 July, respectively, in order to take up seats in the European Parliament, to which they have recently been elected.

(2) In accordance with Article 246, third paragraph, of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, the Council may, acting unanimously on a proposal from the President of the Commission, decide that a vacancy caused in the Commission by resignation need not be filled, in particular when the remainder of the Member’s term of office is short.

(3) From the day the resignations took effect the remainder of the term of office of the current Commission is less than four months. A replacement for such a short period of the Members of the Commission who have resigned does not appear necessary, taking into account the organisation of the Commission as a collegial body pursuant to Article 17(6) of the Treaty on European Union.

(4) This Decision is without prejudice to the composition of the Commission as of the beginning of its new term of office in 2024,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

The vacancies caused in the Commission by the resignations of Commissioner VĂLEAN and Commissioner SINKEVIČIUS shall not be filled.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Done at Brussels, 24 July 2024.

For the Council

The President

BÓKA J.
