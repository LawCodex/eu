# Council Decision of 13 September 2024 adopting the Council’s position on draft amending budget No 2 of the European Union for the financial year 2024

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 13 September 2024**

**adopting the Council’s position on draft amending budget No 2 of the European Union for the financial year 2024**

(C/2024/5532)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 314 thereof, in conjunction with the Treaty establishing the European Atomic Energy Community, and in particular Article 106a thereof,

Having regard to Regulation (EU, Euratom) 2018/1046 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 July 2018 on the financial rules applicable to the general budget of the Union, amending Regulations (EU) No 1296/2013, (EU) No 1301/2013, (EU) No 1303/2013, (EU) No 1304/2013, (EU) No 1309/2013, (EU) No 1316/2013, (EU) No 223/2014, (EU) No 283/2014, and Decision No 541/2014/EU and repealing Regulation (EU, Euratom) No 966/2012 (1), and in particular Article 44 thereof,

Whereas:

– the Union’s budget for the financial year 2024 was definitively adopted on 22 November 2023 (2);

– on 9 April 2024, the Commission submitted a proposal containing draft amending budget No 2 to the general budget for the financial year 2024,

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:

## Sole Article

The Council’s position on draft amending budget No 2 of the European Union for the financial year 2024 was adopted on 13 September 2024.

The full text is available for consultation or download on the Council’s website: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/documents-publications/public-register/public-register-search/.

Done at Brussels, 13 September 2024.

For the Council

The President

BÓKA J.
