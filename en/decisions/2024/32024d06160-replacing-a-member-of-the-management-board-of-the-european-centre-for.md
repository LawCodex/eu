# Council Decision of 8 October 2024 replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Slovenia

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 8 October 2024**

**replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Slovenia**

(C/2024/6160)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/128 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 January 2019 establishing a European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) and repealing Council Regulation (EEC) No 337/75 (1), and in particular Article 4 thereof,

Whereas:

(1) By Decision of 28 March 2023 (2), the Council appointed members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training for the period from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2027.

(2) A seat as member in the government representatives category has fallen vacant as a result of the resignation of Ms Slavica ČERNOŠA.

(3) The Government of Slovenia has designated a candidate for the vacant seat,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Mr Aleksandar SLADOJEVIĆ is appointed member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training in place of Ms Slavica ČERNOŠA for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 March 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Luxembourg, 8 October 2024.

For the Council

The President

VARGA M.

(1) OJ L 30, 31.1.2019, p. 90.

(2) Council Decision of 28 March 2023 appointing members and alternate members of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) (OJ C 116, 31.3.2023, p. 15).
