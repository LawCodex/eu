# Council Decision of 29 January 2024 appointing the Chairperson of the European Statistical Governance Advisory Board

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 29 January 2024**

**appointing the Chairperson of the European Statistical Governance Advisory Board**

(C/2024/1283)

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

Having regard to Decision No 235/2008/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 March 2008 establishing the European Statistical Governance Advisory Board (1), and in particular Article 3 thereof,

Having regard to the opinion of the European Commission,

Having regard to the approval of the European Parliament,

Whereas:

(1) By its Decision of 18 December 2020 (2), the Council appointed Mr Aurel SCHUBERT as Chairperson of the European Statistical Governance Advisory Board for a period of three years from 1 February 2021.

(2) The term of office of Mr Aurel SCHUBERT will expire on 31 January 2024. It is therefore necessary to appoint a new Chairperson to take office upon expiry of that term of office.

(3) Article 3(3) of Decision No 235/2008/EC states that the Chairperson is not to be a current member of either a National Statistical Office or the Commission,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Article 1

Mr Alfred CAMILLERI is hereby appointed as Chairperson of the European Statistical Governance Advisory Board for a period of three years from 1 February 2024.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.

Done at Brussels, 29 January 2024.

For the Council

The President

H. LAHBIB

(1) OJ L 73, 15.3.2008, p. 17.

(2) Council Decision of 18 December 2020 appointing the Chairperson of the European Statistical Governance Advisory Board (OJ C 445, 22.12.2020, p. 1).
