# Council Decision of 22 July 2024 replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Latvia

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 22 July 2024**

**replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Latvia**

**COUNCIL DECISION**

**of 22 July 2024**

**replacing a member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for Latvia**

C/2024/4883

## Article 1

Ms Baiba BAŠĶERE is appointed member of the Management Board of the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training in place of Ms Rūta GINTAUTE-MARIHINA for the remainder of the current term of office, ending on 31 March 2027.

## Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its adoption.
