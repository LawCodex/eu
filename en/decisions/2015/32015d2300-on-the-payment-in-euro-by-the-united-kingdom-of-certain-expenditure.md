**COMMISSION DECISION (EU) 2015/2300**

**of 8 December 2015**

**on the payment in euro by the United Kingdom of certain expenditure resulting from sectoral agricultural legislation**

**(notified under document C(2015) 8576)**

**(Only the English text is authentic)**

## Article 1

The measures communicated by the United Kingdom on 30 October 2015 concerning the payment in euro of expenditure resulting from sectoral agricultural legislation listed in the Annex and, where applicable, any delegated or implementing acts adopted on the basis of those instruments, are hereby approved.

## Article 2

Decision (EU) 2015/1352 is repealed.

## Article 3

This Decision is addressed to the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland.
